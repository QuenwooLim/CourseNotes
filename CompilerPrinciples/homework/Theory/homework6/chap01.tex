\chapter{Exercise 6.1}

\section{Problem}

Consider the grammar:
\[
S \to (L) \;|\; a
\]
\[
L \to L , S \;|\; S
\]

We have the following operator-precedence relations for the grammar:

\begin{table}[htbp]
	\centering
	\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
		\hline
		& a & ( & ) & , & \$ \\
		\hline
		a & & & $>$ & $>$ & $>$ \\
		\hline
		( & $<$ & $<$ & $\equiv$ & $<$ & \\
		\hline
		) & & & $>$ & $>$ & $>$ \\
		\hline
		, & $<$ & $<$ & $>$ & $>$ & \\
		\hline
		\$ & $<$ & $<$ & & & \\
		\hline
	\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Show the detailed process of the parsing of the sentence \textbf{(a, (a, a))}, follow the style in the previous slides.
