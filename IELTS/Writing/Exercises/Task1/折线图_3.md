## Corrected Essay

The line graph compares the consumption of fish and the other three different kinds of meat in a European country over a 25-year period.

In 1979, beef was the most widely consumed type of meat, at nearly four times the figure for fish, which accounted for the smallest proportion. At the same time, the figures for chicken and lamb consumption were about 150 grams per person per week.

Chicken consumption overtook lamb and beef in 1980 and 1989 respectively, and even reached 250 grams per person per week in 2004, making it the dominant meat type. Meanwhile, beef and lamb decreased by nearly 100 grams per person per week from 1979 to 2004, showing a dramatic downward trend.

Although the only significant growth was seen in chicken consumption, fish consumption remained at around 50 grams per person per week, with only a slight downward trend. In 2004, this figure was very close to that of lamb consumption.
