# Grammar & Spelling Check

## Case 1

### Original:

The line graph compare and predict the consumption of energy in USA using six different source from 1980 to 2030.

### Corrected:

The line graph **compares** and **predicts** energy consumption in **the USA** using six different **sources** from 1980 to 2030.

### Reason:

* **compare → compares**: subject-verb agreement.
* **predict → predicts**: verb consistency.
* **USA → the USA**: article required.
* **source → sources**: plural noun needed.
* **consumption of energy → energy consumption**: more natural academic phrasing.

### Reason:

* **Petro → petrol**: spelling correction.
* **is → are**: plural subject.

# Corrected Essay

The line graph compares and predicts energy consumption in the USA using six different fuel sources from 1980 to 2030.

It is clear that petrol and oil are the most important fuels and show an overall upward trend, while hydropower provides the lowest amount of energy consumption for most of the period.

In 1980, petrol and oil were the largest energy source, at 35 quadrillion units, and the figure rose to nearly 40 quadrillion units in 2005. It is expected to reach about 48 quadrillion units by 2030. An upward trend can also be seen in coal and natural gas consumption. They remained at similar levels between 1980 and 2020, but coal is expected to surpass natural gas in the future.

The minor fuel sources include nuclear, solar/wind, and hydropower, which remained relatively stable at between 3 and 8 quadrillion units throughout the 50-year period. Although these sources were at similar levels in 1980, nuclear energy and solar/wind show a gradual increase, whereas hydropower remains stable overall.
