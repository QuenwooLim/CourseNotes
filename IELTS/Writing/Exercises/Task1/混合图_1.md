### Grammar & Spelling Check

#### Case 1

Original: between during the year before refurbishment and during the year after refurbishment

Corrected: **during the year before refurbishment and during the year after refurbishment**

Reason: “Between” is unnecessary and creates a grammatical error.

#### Case 2

Original: visitors **of** Ashdown Museum

Corrected: visitors **to** Ashdown Museum

Reason: The correct preposition is “to” when referring to visitors visiting a place.

#### Case 3

Original: satisfified

Corrected: **satisfied**

Reason: Spelling mistake.

#### Case 4

Original: with higher percentage

Corrected: with **a** higher percentage

Reason: Missing article before a singular countable noun.

#### Case 5

Original: What's more

Corrected: **Moreover**

Reason: “Moreover” is more suitable for formal academic writing.

#### Case 6

Original: the proportion of visitors who were very satisfied with the museum **is** 15% before

Corrected: the proportion of visitors who were very satisfied with the museum **was** 15% before refurbishment

Reason: Past tense should be used consistently.

#### Case 7

Original: rose by 20%

Corrected: **rose to 35%**

Reason: The figure increased from 15% to 35%, so “rose to” is more accurate than “rose by.”

#### Case 8

Original: As for who were satisfied with Ashdown Museum

Corrected: As for **visitors** who were satisfied with Ashdown Museum

Reason: Missing noun after “As for.”

#### Case 9

Original: the proportion of visitors were dissatisfied

Corrected: the proportion of visitors **who were** dissatisfied

Reason: Relative clause is needed for grammatical accuracy.

#### Case 10

Original: saw **an dramatic decreasing**

Corrected: saw **a dramatic decrease**

Reason: Incorrect article and word form.

#### Case 11

Original: only account for 15% and 5% separately

Corrected: only **accounting** for 15% and 5% respectively

Reason: Correct participle form and more appropriate vocabulary.

#### Case 12

Original: after refurbishing

Corrected: after **the refurbishment**

Reason: More natural and accurate noun form.
