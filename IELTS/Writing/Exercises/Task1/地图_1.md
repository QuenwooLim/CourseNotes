### Corrected Essay

The two pictures illustrate the current layout of a university's sports centre and the future plans to redevelop it.

It is noticeable that the current university sports centre is divided into three rectangular sections, while the outdoor courts are planned to be removed in the future. By adding a wider range of facilities, the indoor area of the sports centre will be expanded significantly.

The most noticeable change between the current and future layouts is that the outdoor courts will disappear after the indoor area is enlarged, resulting in a greater total indoor space. Most of the existing indoor facilities will remain unchanged, apart from the gym, which will be extended. The entrance and reception area will also remain in the same position, although visitors will be able to access more facilities from there.

Several new facilities will be added to the sports centre, including two dance studios, two changing rooms, a café, a sports shop, and a leisure pool. The changing rooms will be located in the southern corners of the building, while the café and sports shop will be situated near the entrance. In addition, the largest new facility will be the leisure pool, which will be installed adjacent to the existing changing room on the western side of the centre.
