### Grammar & Spelling Check

#### Case 1

Original: The bar chart **compare**

Corrected: The bar chart **compares**

Reason: Subject-verb agreement error. “Bar chart” is singular, so the verb should be “compares”.

#### Case 2

Original: the percentage of **Australian**

Corrected: the percentage of **Australians**

Reason: A plural noun is needed because it refers to people in general.

#### Case 3

Original: The proportion of **male**

Corrected: The proportion of **males**

Reason: “Male” should be plural when referring to a group of people.

#### Case 4

Original: that of **female**

Corrected: that of **females**

Reason: Parallel structure requires both nouns to be plural.

#### Case 5

### Corrected Essay

The bar chart compares the percentages of Australians in different age groups between men and women who did regular physical activity in 2010.

The proportion of males doing regular physical activity was higher than that of females in the age group between 15 and 24. However, the percentage of Australian men who engaged in regular physical activity was lower for all age groups aged 25 and over.

Males who were between 15 and 24 years old recorded the highest proportion in 2010 at 52.8%, while 53.3% of women in the 45 to 54 age group did regular physical activity. Around 47% of Australians who were over 65 years old engaged in physical activity regularly.

The age group 35 to 44 recorded the largest difference between men and women during the whole year, and the percentage of women was the lowest at the same time. In addition, the average proportion of males was higher than that of females in 2010.
