### Corrected Essay

People's dressing styles are influenced by global fashion trends in many aspects. I completely agree with the view that it is positive for global fashion to greatly influence people's lives.

With the development of society and technology, people are able to communicate with each other easily all over the world. Therefore, global culture and information, especially fashion, have spread around the world. For example, I am a big fan of social media, and the internet is the most useful tool for me to get the latest information so that I am able to follow global fashion trends. What's more, it is online shopping that makes people search for products and order the clothes they want more easily. These are the reasons why global fashion has become such a strong influence.

Although global fashion trends may have negative effects on people's lives in some cases, I strongly believe that they are a positive development. It is important for people to keep pace with global trends so that they can be aware of worldwide developments instead of becoming isolated. Following global trends can broaden our horizons and open our minds; it is not only about clothing. As for me, I gain a lot of pleasure and satisfaction from trying different kinds of clothes, especially traditional and global fashion styles. Therefore, it undoubtedly plays a positive role.

In conclusion, my view is that people's lives have been greatly influenced by global fashion due to the development of science and technology, and this situation is definitely a positive development.
