## Corrected Essay

Governments invest large amounts of money in different sectors, especially transportation. Although railways are an efficient form of transport, I completely disagree with the idea that governments should spend more money on railways than roads. In my opinion, authorities should maintain a balance between investment in railways and road systems.

There is no doubt that governments should invest in railways because they are efficient and convenient for long-distance transportation. With the development of science and technology, more and more railways and subway systems have been constructed, allowing people to travel between cities at high speed. For example, I used to travel from my hometown to college by car, and the journey took about six hours. However, thanks to high-speed railways, the same trip now takes only one hour. Therefore, railways significantly reduce travel time and improve transportation services.

Although railway construction costs a considerable amount of money, roads are equally important in modern cities. The road transport system plays a crucial role in carrying goods and passengers on a daily basis. In addition, many people still commute by car rather than by train or subway. As a result, governments should continue investing in road infrastructure to meet public demand and maintain an efficient transportation network.

To sum up, I strongly believe that governments should not focus only on railways while ignoring roads. Instead, they should develop both systems equally in order to create a balanced and effective transportation network.
