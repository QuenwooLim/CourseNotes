## Corrected Essay

With the development of the economy and society, companies use various advertising methods to promote their products and emphasize that they are new in some way. This is mainly because businesses want to attract customers' attention in a highly competitive market. However, I strongly believe that this is a negative development, and companies should focus more on the functions and practical value of their products.

There are several reasons why businesses emphasize novelty in advertisements. Firstly, consumers are often interested in new products because they believe these items are more fashionable or advanced. Secondly, companies can increase sales more easily by creating a sense of excitement and curiosity among customers. As a result, emphasizing novelty has become an effective marketing strategy in modern society.

However, this development has many negative effects. To begin with, consumers may not clearly understand the practical functions and uses of products if advertisements focus too much on novelty. In addition, exaggerated advertising can mislead people into buying unnecessary products without careful consideration, which wastes money. Furthermore, the overuse of certain products may even cause side effects that are harmful to people's health. Therefore, businesses should advertise products in a more responsible and informative way.

In conclusion, businesses emphasize the novelty of products mainly to attract customers and increase profits. Nevertheless, I believe this is a negative development because it may encourage unnecessary consumption and mislead consumers. Companies should focus more on the quality, functions, and practical value of their products.
