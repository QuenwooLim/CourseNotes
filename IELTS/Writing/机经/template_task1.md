#### 2. 表示上升/下降的语句

以下是对该图表内容的 Markdown 格式整理（不使用表格，按类别分段呈现）：

**动词**

### Process Diagram / Flow Chart

#### 1. 工序图/生长图

- **工序图**: 一般是描述某类产品是怎么制作的、服务是怎么提供的
  - 以**被动**语态为主
  - 一般不会出现
  - 有时候一道工序会有几种加工的方法，需要对比
  - 状语一般是工具
- **生物生长图**: 一般是描述某种生物是怎么成长的。
  - 以**主动**语态比较多，也有被动 
  - 在生物生长的每个阶段都可能会描述 
  - 主要是不同阶段生物个体（如大小、行为）的对比
  - 状语一般是时间或地点

#### 2. 常用词语表达

- 阶段: stage, step, phase
- 第几步:
  - 最初: at the first/initial/early
  - 下一步: enter/reach the next/following/subsequent/second stage
  - 最后: at the final/last stage
  - 连接词: then, next, finally 等
- 总述: 
  - go through/pass through/undergo/experience five stages
  - the process consists of five stages
  - the process can be divided into five stages

#### 3. 步骤连接常用句式

- 用 where 或者 in which 引导的定语从句

*eg: Chocolate is sent to the oven, where it is heated and turned into liquid.*

- 使用 before 或者 after+分词的结构

*eg: Chocolate is sent to the oven, before being heated and turned into liquid.*

- 使用 after which 引导的定语从句

*eg: Chocolate is sent to the oven at the first stage, after which it is heated and turned into liquid.*

- 使用 until, once 引导的状语语从句

*eg: Chocolate is placed in the oven, until it is heated and turned into liquid.*

- 使用 and 连接两个步骤

*eg: Chocolate is heated in the oven and turned into liquid.*

### Map

#### 1. 常用句式

1. located/situated/sited/built/constructed+地点

    *eg: A wetland is located to the southeast of the lake.*

2. 地点+with+地点+方位

    *eg: This region has large woodland with a farm to the south.*

3. there be 句型

    *eg: There are two schools on the south-eastern corner of the area.*

4. 使用 while, but 和 and 连接的句子

    *eg: A mountain **is located to** the north of the city, while there is a river to the south.*

5. 倒装句

    *eg: To the south of the river was a library.*

6. 使用名词性从句

- it is noticeable/clear that, 
- it should be noted that, 
- one significant change is that

    *eg: It is noticeable that the library has a row of meeting rooms on the eastern side.*

#### 2. 变化：

变化包括两种:图形原有事物的改变，图形新添事物。

1. 总体表述

    *eg: The map shows the development of the village of Kelsbey between 1780 and 2000.*

2. 原有事物词语描述 original/previous/former + 事物

    *eg: The original/previous/former garden*

3. 原有事物尺寸上变大/变小 enlarged/extended/halved/reduced

    *eg: The size of the library has been enlarged/extended/halved/reduced by half*

4. 原有事物在数量上增多或减少 increased/risen/grown/reduced/decreased/dropped/fallen

    *eg: The number of homes has increased/risen/grown/reduced/decreased/dropped/fallen to 500.*

5. 原有事物消失 disappeared/were removed

    *eg: The farms completely disappeared/were removed.*

6. 原有事物被改为 

- A becomes B
- A is transformed/reconstructed/converted/turned over/changed  to/into B 
- A is replaced/substituted by B 
- A gives way to B

### 6. 图形新添事物的改变

- A newly-built road
- A new car park was built/established/set up/constructed/completed and opened in the middle of B
- A new IT centre has been added to the library
- The year 2000 saw two additions to the land: a pond in the northern part and a vegetable garden on the opposite side.

#### 3. 时态语态

1. 完成时态

    *eg: 现在停车场改成了一个剧院。The car park has been removed and replaced by a theatre.*

2. 被动语态

    *eg: 2000 年在城镇 A 新建了一个医院。A new hospital was established in Town A in 2000.*
