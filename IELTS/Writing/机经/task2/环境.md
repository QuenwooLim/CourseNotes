# 环境

环境问题的成因与解决

There are many environmental problems, such as the greenhouse effect and climate change, and people have hardly found any solutions. Why is this the case? And what measures can be taken?

消费品生产与自然破坏

The increase in the production of consumer goods results in damage to the natural environment. What are the causes of this? What can be done to solve this problem?

污染治理的责任主体

Some people think that companies and individuals should pay to clear up the pollution they produced and that the government should not pay for it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

淡水短缺的成因与解决


Fresh water is a limited resource in some parts of the world. Today, increasing demand made it a global problem. What are the causes of increasing demand, what measures can governments and individuals take to solve this problem?


塑料污染的治理措施

More and more plastic is polluting cities, countryside and oceans. What are the problems caused by plastic waste? What measures should be taken to solve it?


气候变化对商业的影响

Some people think climate change has a negative impact on business, while others think that it provides more business opportunities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.


生活水平与环境破坏

Damage to the environment is an inevitable consequence of the improvement in the standard of living. To what extent do you agree or disagree？


应对气候变化的方式

Some people think that instead of preventing climate change, we need to find a way to live with it. To what extent do you agree or disagree?


野生动物保护的资源投入

Some people think that too much attention and too many resources have been given to the protection of wild animals and birds. To what extent do you agree or disagree?


环境问题的解决主体探讨

Some people think that environmental problems are too big for individuals to solve. Others, however, believe that these problems cannot be solved if individuals do not take actions. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.


偏远地区能源开采影响

With the increasing demand for energy sources of oil and gas, people should look for sources of oil and gas in remote and untouched natural places. Do the advantages outweigh disadvantages of damaging such areas？

偏远自然环境旅行影响

Scientists and tourists can travel to remote natural environments such as the South Pole. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?


自然资源消耗问题解决

Natural resources such as oil, forests and fresh water are being consumed at an alarming rate. What problems does this cause? How can we solve these problems?


保护动物与关注人类问题

Many animal species in the world are becoming extinct nowadays. Some people say that countries and individuals should protect these animals from dying out, while others say we should concentrate more on problems of human beings. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
