Many countries are still **living in poverty**, and some people believe that richer countries should provide poorer nations with other forms of assistance **rather than simply offering financial aid**. Overall, I completely agree that rich countries should provide poorer countries with **other forms of assistance in addition to financial aid**, because this approach can help them achieve sustainable development more effectively.

## 名人助力国际援助的影响

Some people believe famous people's support towards international aid organizations draws(引起) the attention to problems, while others think celebrities make the problems less important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

有些人认为，名人对国际援助组织的支持能够引起人们对相关问题的关注；而另一些人则认为，名人的参与反而会让这些问题显得没那么重要。请讨论这两种观点并给出你自己的看法。

### Reasoning 

- Intro: There are many celebrities support international aid organizations on social media. Some people think they make the problems less important, while I completely agree that the behaviour draws the attention to problems.
- Body 1: The focus is moved from the urgent internation problems to the celebrities.
- Body 2: the behaviour draws the attention to problems, which makes more people pay attention to international issues
- Summary: I strongly hold the view that famous people's support plays an important in addressing international issues

### Answer

Many celebrities actively support international aid organizations through social media and public **campaigns**. While some people argue that **celebrity involvement** can **distract attention from serious global issues**, I believe that it is **an effective way to raise public awareness and encourage support for these causes.**

Some people believe that celebrities make international problems seem less important. One reason is that **media coverage often focuses more on the celebrity than on the issue itself.** As a result, the public may pay more attention to a famous person's appearance, lifestyle, or personal opinions than to the humanitarian crisis being discussed. Moreover, complex international problems may be **oversimplified** when they **are presented through social media posts**. **Consequently**, people may fail to understand the seriousness and complexity of these issues.

However, I strongly believe that celebrity support plays a valuable role in drawing attention to international problems. Famous people often have millions of followers and enjoy significant influence over the public. **Their involvement can help spread information about issues that many people would otherwise ignore.** Furthermore, **celebrity campaigns** can encourage people to donate money, volunteer their time, or support charitable projects. As a result, **celebrity involvement** not only **raises awareness** but also **generates practical assistance for those in need.** In many cases, international aid organizations **receive greater public attention and resources** because of **celebrity endorsements**.

In conclusion, although celebrity involvement may **sometimes shift attention away from the issues themselves**, I believe that the advantages outweigh the disadvantages. Their influence can significantly **increase public awareness, encourage public participation, and provide valuable support** for international aid organizations in addressing global challenges.
