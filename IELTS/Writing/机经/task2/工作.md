## 背井离乡找工作的影响

### Question

In many countries, people need to leave their families and friends to find jobs in other places. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

在许多国家，人们需要离开家人和朋友，去其他地方寻找工作。你认为这样做利大于弊吗？

### Reasoning

- Intro: 很多人需要离开家人和朋友去其他地方寻找工作。虽然离开了自己长期生活的环境，但是有机会获得更好的就业机会。总体上我认为利大于弊
- Body 1: 坏处
  - 离开了自己长期生活的环境，失去了家人和朋友的支持 -> loss of family support, loneliness, reduced emotional stability
  - 需要花费很多时间和精力去适应新环境 -> adjust to new environment, language barriers, culturall differences
  - 可能无法适应新的环境，有语言障碍、心理压力问题 --^
  - -> 社交重建困难 building a new social network take time and effort
- Body 2: 好处
  - 有机会获得更好的就业机会，提高收入水平 -> access to larger job markets, high salay, better career progression(前程)
  - 改善原来的生活环境，获得更好的生活质量 -> enjoy better living infrastructure, improved healthcare and education, higher income enabling better living standards
  - 可以学到新的技能和知识，拓宽视野 -> learn new skills, develop independence, broaden horizons
- Summary

有些人认为年轻人应该可以自由地选择工作，而另一些人则认为他们应该现实一点，多为自己的未来考虑。请讨论这两种观点并给出你自己的看法。

### Reasoning

- Intro: As for choosing a job, some people think young people can choose the jobs they like, while others focus on the future prospects. ~~I think young people should be free to choose their job if they can try their best to be responsible for the consequences.~~ I believe they should generally have the freedom to pursue their interests, as long as they are aware of the responsibilities that come with their choices.
