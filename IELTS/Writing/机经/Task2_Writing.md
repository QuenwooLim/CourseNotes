### 1. 犯罪惩处与教育的选择

#### Question

In most countries, prison is an effective solution for the problem of crime. Some people think a more effective solution is to provide a better education. To what extent do you agree or disagree？

#### Reasoning
- **Intro**: The debate over whether imprisonment or education is a more effective solution to crime is ongoing. I largely agree that education and vocational training are superior for long-term rehabilitation, although prisons remain necessary for severe offenders to ensure public safety.
- **Body 1: 为什么教育和培训是更有效的解决方式 (支持观点)**
  - 监狱往往无法实现改造目的，轻犯在狱中可能受重刑犯影响，出狱后更容易再次犯罪 prisons often fail to rehabilitate; minor offenders may be negatively influenced by serious felons and are more likely to reoffend
  - 缺乏生存技能是犯罪的主因，教育能提供专业技能，帮助罪犯重新融入社会 lack of survival skills is a primary cause of crime; education provides professional skills to help offenders reintegrate into society
- **Body 2: 监狱依然具有不可替代的作用 (让步/补充)**
  - 监狱通过剥夺自由，对潜在犯罪分子起到强烈的威慑作用 imprisonment serves as a strong deterrent force against potential criminals by depriving them of freedom
  - 对于对社会有严重危害的罪犯（如杀人、抢劫），必须通过监禁来保障公众安全 severe offenders posing substantial threats to society must be incarcerated to ensure public safety
- **Summary**

### 2. 反社会行为的成因与解决

#### Question

Today there is a great increase in anti-social behavior and lack of respect to others. What are the causes of this? What measures can be taken to reduce this problem?

#### Reasoning
- **Intro**: The alarming rise in antisocial behavior and disrespect towards others has sparked widespread concern. This issue is primarily driven by dysfunctional family environments and negative media influence, but can be mitigated through targeted educational and regulatory measures.
- **Body 1: 导致反社会行为的主要原因**
  - 家庭教育缺失与不当管教：父母忽视、虐待或采用强制性体罚，导致孩子缺乏同理心并产生攻击性 family upbringing deficits: neglect, abuse, or coercive discipline lead to a lack of empathy and aggressive tendencies in children
  - 社会环境与媒体的负面引导：部分媒体对暴力行为的美化以及同伴压力，扭曲了年轻人的价值观 societal environment and media: glamorization of violence and peer pressure distort young people's values
- **Body 2: 减少该问题的有效措施**
  - 强化家庭与学校的道德教育：父母应树立积极榜样，学校需加强心理健康和尊重他人的引导 strengthen moral education at home and school: parents should set positive role models, and schools must guide mental health and respect for others
  - 政府加强媒体监管与社会支持：限制暴力内容的传播，并为边缘化群体提供社区支持项目 government regulation and social support: restrict violent content and provide community support programs for marginalized groups
- **Summary**

### 3. 违法者的惩处方式选择

#### Question

Some people think that lawbreakers should be sent to prison. However, others think that better talents among those should be made to work. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

#### Reasoning
- **Intro**: Opinions differ on whether criminals should be incarcerated or required to perform community work. In my view, while prison is essential for dangerous criminals, community service is a far better alternative for minor offenders and juveniles.
- **Body 1: 将罪犯送入监狱的合理性 (支持监禁)**
  - 监禁能有效隔离危险分子，防止他们在社会中继续作恶 incarceration effectively isolates dangerous individuals, preventing them from causing further harm in society
  - 失去自由的痛苦对潜在的犯罪分子具有强大的威慑力 the pain of being deprived of freedom serves as a powerful deterrent against potential lawbreakers
- **Body 2: 让罪犯参与劳动/社区服务的优势 (支持替代方案)**
  - 对于轻微犯罪者或青少年，社区服务能避免他们沾染监狱中的不良风气，有助于心理矫正 for minor offenders or juveniles, community service avoids exposure to bad habits in prison and aids psychological correction
  - 通过无偿劳动（如清扫街道、照顾老人），罪犯能直接回馈社会，培养责任感和法律意识 unpaid labor allows offenders to give back to society directly, cultivating a sense of responsibility and legal awareness
- **Summary**

### 💡 使用小贴士
*   **搭配使用：** 比如在写“**政府应该怎么做**”时，可以用 **"enforce stringent regulations"** (实施严格法规)。
*   **替换词汇：** 将普通的 "bad thing" 替换为 **"drawback"**, **"downside"**, 或 **"pitfall"**；将 "good thing" 替换为 **"benefit"**, **"advantage"**, 或 **"boon"**。
*   **连接逻辑：** 在列举原因时用 **"stem from"** (源于) 或 **"be attributed to"** (归因于)；在列举结果时用 **"result in"**, **"lead to"**, 或 **"contribute to"**。
