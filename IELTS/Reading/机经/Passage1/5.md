Section A  A decibel Hell:

A 分贝环境
It’s not difficult for a person to encounter sound at levels that can cause adverse health effects. During a single day, people living in a typical urban environment can experience a wide range of sounds in many locations, even once-quiet beaks have become polluted with noise. In fact, it’s difficult today to escape sound completely. In its 1999 Guidelines for Community Noise, the World Health Organization (WHO) declared, “Worldwide, noise-induced hearing impairment is the most prevalent irreversible occupational hazard, and it is estimated that 120 million people worldwide have disabling hearing difficulties.” Growing evidence also points to many other health effects of too much volume.
在平时的日常生活中，各种噪音随处可见，它们会对人体造成不良影响。 生活在典型城市环境中的人们一天中可能在许多地方体验到各种各样的声音，曾经安静的乡村也受到了噪音污染。事实上，我们今天很难完全脱离声音的影响。世界卫生组织(WHO)在1999年的《社区噪音指南》中宣称:“在全世界范围内，噪音引起的听力障碍是最普遍的不可逆转的职业危害，据估计，全世界有1.2亿人丧失听力能力。”越来越多的证据也指出了噪音会对人体健康产生许多其他负面影响。
Mark Stephenson, a Cincinnati, Ohio-based senior research audiologist at the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH), says his agency’s definition of hazardous noise is sound that exceeds the time-weighted average of 85 dB, meaning the average noise exposure measured over a typical eight-hour work day. Other measures and definitions are used for other purposes.
位于俄亥俄州辛辛那提的美国国家职业安全与健康研究所(NIOSH)高级研究听力专家马克·斯蒂芬森(Mark Stephenson)表示，机构对危险噪声的定义，即超过了85分贝的时间加权平均值，也就是在典型的八小时工作日内测得的噪音暴露量的平均值。其他方法和定义用于其他目的。
Section B  Growing Volume

B 加大音量
In the United States, about 30 million workers are exposed to hazardous sound levels on the job, according to NIOSH. Industries having a high number of workers exposed to loud sounds include construction, agriculture, mining, manufacturing, utilities, transportation, and the military.
NIOSH的数据显示，美国约有3000万工人在工作时暴露于危险的音量中。 在一些行业里，大量工人暴露在剧烈的响声中，这些行业包括建筑业，农业，采矿，制造业，公用事业，运输和军事。
Noise in U.S. industry is an extremely difficult problem to monitor, acknowledges Craig Moulton, a senior industrial hygienist for the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA). “Still” he says, “OSHA does require that any employer with workers overexposed to noise provide protection for those employees against the harmful effects of noise. Additionally, employers must implement a continuing, effective hearing conservation program as outlined in OSHA’s Noise Standard.”
美国职业安全与健康管理局(OSHA)高级工业保健专家克雷格·莫尔顿(Craig Moulton)表示，美国的工业噪音的确难以监测。他说，“OSHA确实要求所有雇主为深受噪音之苦的员工提供保护措施，以免受到噪音影响。”此外，雇主必须按照OSHA的噪音标准，实施持续有效的听力保护计划。
Section C  Scary Sound Effects

C 可怕音效
Numerous scientific studies over the years have confirmed that exposure to certain levels of sound can damage hearing. Pronged exposure can actually change the structure of the hair cells in the inner ear, resulting in hearing loss. It can also cause tinnitus, a ringing, roaring, buzzing, or clicking in the ears.
多年来许多科学研究证实，暴露在一定程度的噪音环境中会损害听力。长时间的暴露实际上会改变内耳毛细胞的结构，导致听力下降。同时也会造成耳鸣现象，即耳朵内会有铃响或轰鸣或嗡嗡或滴答的声音。
NIOSH studies from the mid to late 1990s show that 90% of coal miners have hearing impairment by age 52—compared to 9% of the general population—and 70% of male metal/nonmetal miners will experience hearing impairment by age 60 (Stephenson notes that from adolescence onward, females tend to have better hearing than males). Neitzel says nearly half of all construction workers have some degree of hearing loss. “NIOSH research also reveals that by age twenty-five, the average carpenter’s hearing is equivalent to an otherwise healthy fifty-year-old male who hasn’t been exposed to noise,” he says.
NIOSH在20世纪90年代中期到后期的研究显示, 一般情况下9%的人在52岁的时候会有听力障碍，而这一比例在矿工中达到90%，而70%从事金属/非金属矿业的男性则会在60岁的时候才会遇到以上问题。(斯蒂芬森指出,从青春期开始,女性往往比男性有更好的听力)。Neitzel说，几乎一半的建筑工人都有一定程度的听力问题。他说:“NIOSH的研究还发现，25岁木匠的平均听力相当于一个没有受到任何噪音影响的50岁健康男性的听力”。
William Luxford, medical director of the House Ear Clinic of St. Vincent Medical Center in Los Angeles, points out one piece of good news: “It’s true that continuous noise exposure will lead to the continuation of hearing loss, but as soon as the exposure is stopped, the hearing loss stops. So a change in environment can improve a person’s hearing health.”
洛杉矶圣文森特医疗中心(St. Vincent medical Center)豪斯耳科诊所的医疗主任威廉·卢克斯福德(William Luxford)指出了一条好消息:“持续暴露于噪音环境中会导致听力不断下降，但一旦噪音停止，这种听力损害就会停止。”因此，环境的改变可以改善一个人的听力。
Research is catching up with this anecdotal evidence. In the July 2001 issue of Pediatrics, researchers from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention reported that, based on audiometric testing of 5, 249 children as part of the Third National Health and Nutrition, Examination Survey, an estimated 12.5% of American children have noise-induced hearing threshold shifts — or dulled hearing — in one or both ears. Most children with noise-induced hearing threshold shifts have only limited hearing damage, but continued exposure to excessive noise can lead to difficulties with high-frequency sound discrimination. The report listed stereos, music concerts, toys (such as toy telephones and certain rattles), lawn mowers, and fireworks as producing potentially harmful sounds.
研究正在赶上这些轶事证据。2001年7月出版的儿科学一书中，疾病控制和预防中心的研究人员记录了以下数字：在第三次全国健康和营养检查调查中，5249名儿童参加了听力测试，结果估计有12.5%的美国儿童有一个或两个耳朵出现噪音引起的听力阈值变化—或听觉迟钝的现象。大多数由噪声引起的听力阈值变化的儿童听力损害不大，但持续暴露在高强度的噪声中则会导致难以辨别高频声音。报告指出立体音响、音乐会、玩具(比如玩具电话和拨浪鼓)、割草机和烟花都有可能产生有害的声音。
Section D  Beyond the Ears

D 影响不止于耳朵
The effects of sound don’t stop with the ears. Nonauditory effects of noise exposure are those effects that don’t cause hearing loss but still can be measured, such as elevated blood pressure, loss of sleep, increased heart rate, cardiovascular constriction, labored breathing, and changes in brain chemistry.
声音并不仅仅对耳朵产生影响。暴露在噪音下的非听觉影响不包括听力损失，但包括血压升高、睡眠不足、心率加快、心血管收缩、呼吸困难、脑化学变化，而且这些影响是可以估量出来的。
The nonauditory effects of noise were noted as early as 1930 in a study published by E.L. Smith and D.L. Laird in volume 2 of the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America. The results showed that exposure to noise caused stomach contractions in healthy human beings. Reports on noise’s nonauditory effects published since that pioneering study have been both contradictory and controversial in some areas. Bronzaft and the school principal persuaded the school board to have acoustical tile installed in the classrooms adjacent to the tracks. The Transit Authority also treated the tracks near the school to make them less noisy. A follow-up study published in the September 1981 issue of the Journal of Environmental Psychology found that children’s reading scores improved after these interventions were put in place.
早在1930年，E.L. Smith和D.L. Laird在《美国声学学会杂志》第2卷发表的一项研究中就发现了噪音的非听觉效应。结果显示，暴露于噪音环境中会引起常人胃收缩。噪音的非听觉效应报告发布之后，一些领域里开始出现既矛盾又有争议的开创性研究。Bronzaft和学校校长说服校董会在跑道旁的教室安装声学瓷砖。交通运输管理局还对学校附近的铁轨进行了处理，以减少噪音。发表在1981年9月《环境心理学杂志》上的一项后续研究发现，这些干预措施到位之后，儿童的阅读成绩有所提高。
Section E  Fighting for Quiet

E 反噪音活动
Anti-noise activists say that Europe and several countries in Asia are more advanced than the United States in terms of combating noise. “Population pressure has prompted Europe to move more quickly on the noise issue than the United States has,” Hume says. In the European Union, countries with cities of at least 250, 000 people are creating noise maps of those cities to help leaders determine noise pollution policies.
反噪音活动人士声称，在噪音治理这方面，欧洲和亚洲的几个国家走在美国前面。“人口压力促使欧洲在噪音问题处理上比美国更为迅速，”休谟说。在欧盟，拥有至少25万城市人口的国家正在描绘城市噪音分布图，以帮助领导人确定噪音污染政策治理的方向。
Paris has already prepared its first noise maps. The map data, which must be finished by 2007, will be fed into computer models that will help test the sound impact of street designs or new buildings before construction begins.
巴黎已经开始着手准备了它的第一个噪音地图，地图数据必须在2007年之前完成，然后输入电脑，形成计算机模型，用来帮助测试街道设计的声音影响或是新建筑施工前的声音效果。
Activists in other countries say they too want the United States to play a more leading role on the noise issue. But as in other areas of environmental health, merely having a more powerful government agency in place that can set more regulations is not the ultimate answer, according to other experts. Bronzaft stresses that governments worldwide need to increase funding for noise research and do a better job coordinating their noise pollution efforts so they can establish health and environmental policies based on solid scientific research. “Governments have a responsibility to protect their citizens by curbing noise pollution,” she says.
其他国家的活动人士表示，他们也希望美国在噪音问题上发挥更主要的作用。但其他专家则认为，与其他环境健康领域一样，仅仅拥有一个更强大的政府机构来制定更多的法规并不是最终的解决方案。Bronzaft强调，世界各国政府需要增加对噪音研究的资助，并在协调噪音污染方面做出更多改进，只有这样才能在可靠的科学研究基础上制定健康和环境政策。“政府有责任控制噪音污染来保护他们的公民，”她说。
