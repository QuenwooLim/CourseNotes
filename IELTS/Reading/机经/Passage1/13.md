Building an Ancient Castle in Modern Times

在现代建造一座古堡

A   When Michel Guyot, the owner of the Saint-Fargeau castle in the Burgundy area of France，announced that he was going to totally build a 13th century castle in an area where his 15th century castle had stood, using tools and methods of construction that were available only in the Middle Ages, some found this idea mildly amusing and others dismissed as outright folly. However, Maryline Martin, who became the project director for the construction project，viewed the idea as exciting, one that could regenerate the energy and the economy of the region. Before the project started, various partners in the project had to be secured, including architects, archaeologists, and investors. A site in the heart of Guedelon forest was later found：a site which offered not only all the resources required for building a castle a stone quarry, an oak forest and a water supply―but sufficient enough to satisfy the demands of this massive undertaking. Work on the castle started on June 20, 1997, when the first stone was laid.

当法国勃艮第地区圣法尔若城堡的主人米歇尔·居约宣布他要在他15世纪的城堡矗立的地方使用只有中世纪才有的工具和方法完全建一座13世纪城堡时，一些人觉得这个想法有点好笑，而另一些人则认为这是彻头彻尾的愚蠢。然而，成为了这个建筑项目的项目主管的玛丽琳认为这个想法很令人兴奋，可以复兴该地区能源和经济。在项目开始之前，必须确定项目中的各个合作伙伴，包括建筑师、考古学家和投资者。后来在盖德隆森林的中心找到了一块地：这块地不仅提供了建造一座城堡所需的所有资源——一座采石场、一片橡树林和一处水源，而且还足够满足这项浩大工程的需要。1997年6月20日，第一块石头铺好，城堡开始动工。

B   Unlike owners of any other present-day building site, Michel Guyot welcomes the public to be part of the project. The workers' role is to demonstrate and expand, to a wide audience, the skills of our forefathers. Stone quarrying, the building of vaulted ceilings, the blacksmith's work and the raising of roof timbers are just some of the activities which visitors can witness during a visit to Guedelon. The workers are always on hand to talk about their craft and the progress of the castle, at the cost of falling behind the construction schedule. Guided tours are tailored to the school curriculum and age groups. Pupils of all ages have the opportunity to follow the footsteps of medieval stonemasons by taking part in a stone-carving workshop or discover the secrets of the medieval master-builders at the geometry workshop.

与其他现代建筑的所有者不同，米歇尔·居约欢迎公众参与项目。工人的作用是向广大群众展示和解释我们祖先的技能。石、拱形天花板的建造、铁匠的工作和屋顶木材的抬高都是游客在盖德隆参观期间可以看到的活动。工人们总是在场谈论他们的手艺和城堡的进展，而这么做的代价是落后于建设的时间表。有导游的游览是根据学校的课程和年龄组量身定做的。所有年龄的学生都有机会通过参加石雕工作坊来跟随中世纪石匠的脚步，或在几何工作坊中发现中世纪建筑大师的秘密。

C   Interestingly, this project does not involve restoring an old castle.  Instead, a new “old" castle is being built. Builders are creating a castle from zero. They have been building it for nearly ten years now, but the project is not even halfway finished. Jean Francois, a member of the Guedelon stone cutter's guild, says that he works eight hours a day using a 13th Century chisel and iron mallet.  Apparently, there is no way to speed up without the involvement of modern technology.

有趣的是，这个项目并不包括修复一座古堡。相反，一座新的“旧”城堡正在建造中。建筑工人们正在从零开始建造一座城堡。他们建造这座城堡已经快十年了，但工程还没有完成一半。让·弗朗索瓦是盖德隆石匠协会的成员，他说自己每天用13世纪的凿子和铁锤工作八小时。显然，没有现代技术的参与，就没有办法加快速度。

D   Construction is sometimes hindered by the fact that the site is open to tourists during construction. The visitors, however unsightly they may be, are vital to the project.  The initial funding came not from pillaging the local peasantry but from regional councils, the European Union and large companies. For the last 10 years, Guedelon, 100 miles southeast of Paris，has funded itself from its entrance fees. Last year it had a record of 300,000 visitors，who paid almost €2.5m, making it the second most-visited site in Burgundy. The most-visited site was the Hospice de Beaune, a beautiful 15th-century alms-house built 600 years before, or, if you prefer, 200 years “after”，Guedelon.

工地在施工期间对游客开放，这有时会妨碍施工。不管游客有多碍眼，他们对这个项目来说都是至关重要的。最初的资金不是来自对当地农民的掠夺，而是来自地方议会、欧盟和大公司。在过去10年里，位于巴黎东南方向100英里的盖德隆一直靠门票收入为自己提供资金。去年，它的游客达到了创纪录的30万人，他们支付了近250万欧元，使其成为勃艮第游客第二多的景点。参观人数最多的地方是伯恩临终关怀院，这是一座建于600年前，或者可以说是在盖德隆“之后”200年建成的美丽的15世纪济贫院。

E   Limestone is found in the construction of various local buildings, from the great and prestigious edifice of Ratilly castle to the more modest audiences houses. As limestone (containing 30%-40% iron oxide) is extremely difficult to cut and dress, quarrymen need to determine and anticipate the natural fault of the stone before digging a series of angular holes into the block and hammering iron wedges into these holes. The shock waves produced by the quarrymen's sledgehammers cause the stone to split along a straight line. The highest quality blocks are used to produce lintels, voussoirs, corbels, ashlars etc. The medium quality blocks are roughly shaped by the stone cutters and used on the course curtain walls, and as facing stones on the castle's inner walls. There are water-filled clay pits in the forest.  Clay is taken from these pits before being cleaned and plugged. It is then shaped in wooden moulds to form bricks. After the bricks have been left to air-dry, they are placed in a woodfired kiln for about 12 hours, heated at roughly 100℃.

石灰岩存在于各种当地建筑的建造中，从宏伟而有名的拉蒂利城堡大厦到较为朴素的波伊奥迪尼别墅。由于石灰岩含有30%-40%的氧化铁，极难被切割和修饰。采石工们需要确定和预测石头的自然断层线之后，在石块上凿一系列有棱角的洞，然后把铁楔子锤打进这些洞。采石工的大锤所产生的冲击波使石头沿着一条直线裂开。高质量的砌块用于生产过梁、楔形拱石、梁托、琢石等。中等质量的砌块由石匠大致凿成形，用于未铺设的幕墙，以及城堡内墙的饰面石材。森林里有填满水的黏土坑。黏土是从这些坑里挖出来的，经过清洁和搅拌，然后在木模具中成型，形成砖。待砖自然风干后，放入柴火窑中约12小时，在大约100℃的温度下加热。

F   The mortar is the“glue” used to bind the castle's stones. It is made up of precise doses of lime, sand and water. The people working there mix their mortar primarily as they would have done in the 13th century, using sand, instead of the extremely effective hot lime from medieval day, because of its toxicity, and a modern chemical ingredient is applied to achieve the same effect.

灰浆是用来粘合城堡石头的“胶水”在那里它由精确量的石灰、沙子和水组成。工作的人主要像13世纪那样混合砂浆。他们使用沙子，而不是中世纪极度高效的热石灰，因为它的毒性，所以使用了现代化学成分来达到同样的效果。

G   We also learned that even if there is a straight lintel across a doorway, an arch of stones is also used to create further support. The physics of an arch, which channels the weight above it down into whatever is supporting it at each side instead of pressing down in the middle, helps to take a  lot of weight off the lintel itself, whether it is free standing or buried in the wall against the impact of warfare. The arch is the strongest element for spanning space in stone architecture. This is why, in ancient ruins, arched windows and doorways will remain standing even though entire walls are missing or down.

我们还了解到，即使在门口有一条笔直的过梁，还是用了石头拱起进一步的支撑作用。拱门的物理特性使上面的重量被导到在下面两侧支持的任何物体，而不是压在中间，这有助于减少过梁本身的重量，无论它是独立式的或是为了抵抗战争的影响而埋在墙里。在石头建筑中，拱是跨越空间的最强有力的元素。这就是为什么在古代的废墟中，即使整个墙壁都消失或倒塌了，拱形的窗户和门道仍然矗立着。
