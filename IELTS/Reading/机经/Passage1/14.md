Ambergris

龙涎香

What is it and where does it come from?

是什么，来源是在哪里？

A Ambergris remains a mystical matter for perfumers. Almost every civilization on the earth has been fascinated by the power of ambergris. In about 2000 BC, the Chinese already knew ambergris and named it as lung sien hiang, meaning “perfume of the dragon drool”. Back then, the Chinese used it in pharmacy against the respiratory diseases and saved many lives.  They were also the first to use it as herbs in the kitchen for culinary purposes. The Arabs knew ambergris as amber, believing that it is produced from springs under seas. During the Middle Ages, Europeans invented a remedy based on ambergris for headaches, colds，epilepsy, and other illnesses.

龙涎香对香水师来说仍然是一个神秘的东西。几乎地球上所有的文明都被龙涎香的力量所吸引。大约在公元前2000年，中国人就已经知道龙涎香了，并把它命名为龙涎香，意思是“龙唾液的香水”。当时，中国人把它用于治疗呼吸系统疾病，挽救了许多人的生命。他们也是第一批将其作为药草用于厨房烹饪的人。阿拉伯人称龙涎香为安巴尔，认为它是海底泉水的产物。在中世纪，欧洲人利用龙涎香发明了一种治疗头痛、感冒、癫痫和其他疾病的药物。

B In the 1851 whaling novel Moby-Dick, Herman Melville claimed that ambergris was “largely used in perfumery”. But nobody ever knew where it really came from. People from all walks of life tried different searching methods，but still remained unsure of the originality.  At various times, ambergris has been found in some strange and surprising places.  In September 1908, the Hartford Courant reported a lucky find by a fisherman who discovered that one of his lobster traps contained a one-pound piece of ambergris. A year later, a news agency described the moment that the crew of the Hockomock, on its return to Boston, opened up one of several swordfish taken on the Georges Bank and discovered a large piece of ambergris inside it It was noted that the piece was estimated to be worth $20, 000 at that time.

在1851年的捕鲸小说《白鲸记》中，赫尔曼·梅尔维尔声称龙涎香“主要用于香水” 。但没人知道它到底是从哪里来的。各行各业的人们尝试了不同的搜索方法，但仍然不确定其来源。在不同的时期，龙涎香在一些奇怪和令人惊讶的地方被发现。1908年9月，《哈特福德报》报道了一个渔民的幸运发现，他的捕虾器里有一块一磅重的龙涎香。一年后，一家新闻机构描述了“霍克摩克‘’号船员在返回波士顿的途中切开了在乔治海岸捕获的几条剑鱼中的一条的那一刻，他们发现里面有一大块龙涎香。有人指出，这一块当时估计价值20000美元。

C People have been searching out prices of ambergris on sandy beaches for countess ages.  Its name, meaning "grey amber” distinguishes it from golden amber, another priceless treasure. Both types of amber are highly sough-after and are comparable in price to gold — ambergris  sells for roughly $20 per gram compared to gold at $30 per gram.  Amber floats in salt water and the origins of both types of amber were mysterious to people in the past, but it turns out that amber and ambergris have little in common.  Amber is resin of trees which has become fossilized over time. It was familiar to Europeans even long before the discovery of America，and was highly sought-after for jewelry. In jewelry, amber is treated as a gem, although it is actually a wholly organic material—the resin of extinct species of trees, usually pines.

多年来，人们一直在沙滩上寻找龙涎香片。它的名字意思是“灰色琥珀”，与另一件无价之宝金琥珀区别开来。这两种琥珀都很受欢迎，价格与黄金相当——龙涎香每克售价约为20美元，而黄金每克售价为30美元。琥珀漂浮在海水中。这两种琥珀的起源在过去对人们来说是神秘的，但事实证明琥珀和龙涎香几乎没有共同点。琥珀是树木的树脂，随着时间的推移变成化石。甚至在美洲被发现之前，它就为欧洲人所熟悉了，并成为珠宝的抢手货。在珠宝中，琥珀被视为宝石，尽管它实际上是一种完全有机的材料——已灭绝树种的树脂，通常是松树。

D From early written records, it seems that ambergris was thought to come from various sources including the droppings of sea birds (possibly because the beaks of squid were sometimes found) , or from the large hives of bees living near the sea. Marco Polo was the first Western chronicler who correctly attributed ambergris to the correct source—the vomit of sperm whales.

从早期的书面记录来看，龙涎香被认为有各种各样的来源，包括海鸟的粪便(可能因为有时会在其中发现乌贼的喙)，或者是生活在海边的大蜂巢里的蜜蜂。马可·波罗是第一个正确地指出龙涎香的来源(抹香鲸的呕吐物)的西方编年史家。

E As sperm whales navigate in the oceans, they often dive down to 2km or more below the sea level to prey on squid, most famously the Giant Squid. It is commonly accepted that ambergris forms in the whale's gut or intestines as the creature attempts to“deal”with squid beaks.  Sperm whales are rather partial to squid, but seemingly struggle to digest the hard, sharp, parrot-like beaks. Traveling down the whale's intestines, the beak gathers bile and hardens to form a large mass. Eventually, the whale will expel the mass by vomiting. The substance, named ambergris, initially has an unpleasant smell, which may be similar to the smell of cow dung. But after float on the salty ocean for about a decade, the dung smell is gone, replaced by a sweet, smooth, musky and pleasant earthy aroma.

当抹香鲸在海洋中前进时，它们通常会潜到海平面以下2000米或更低的地方去捕食乌贼，最著名的是巨型乌贼。普遍认为，龙涎香在鲸鱼试图“应付”乌贼的喙时形成于鲸鱼的肠道。抹香鲸相当偏爱乌贼，但似乎难以消化它坚硬、锋利、像鹦鹉一样的喙。顺着鲸鱼的肠子往下，乌贼的喙收集胆汁，变硬形成一个大团。最终，鲸鱼会通过呕吐来排出这一团。这种名为龙涎香的物质最初有一种难闻的气味，可能类似于牛粪的气味。但在咸咸的海洋上漂浮了大约十年之后，粪便的味道消失了，取而代之的是一种甜美、柔滑、麝香和令人愉悦的泥土香气。

F Since ambergris is derived from animals, naturally a question of ethics rises, and in the case of ambergris, it is very important to consider. Sperm whales are an endangered species, whose population started to decline as far back as the 19th century due to the increasing demand for their highly emollient oil and they are still struggling with recovery from the decline.  During the 1970s, the“Save the Whales”movement brought international recognition to the decline, and many people now assume that the whale number has recovered. This is certainly not the case. Whaling still exists around the world, with many countries continuing to hunt whales despite international treaties protecting them. Some marine scientists worry that even the trade in ambergris, which is found with no harm to the whales, could provide an extra incentive to hunt whales.

既然龙涎香是源自动物身上，自然就产生了一个道德问题，在龙涎香的例子中，考虑这一点是非常重要的。抹香鲸是一种濒临灭绝的物种，其数量早在19世纪就开始减少，原因是人们对其高软性油的需求不断增加，而抹香鲸的数量仍在艰难地恢复。在20世纪70年代，“拯救鲸鱼”运动引起了国际社会对鲸鱼数量下降的认识，现在许多人认为鲸鱼数量已经恢复。但事实并非如此。世界各地仍有捕鲸活动，许多国家不顾国际条约对鲸鱼的保护，继续捕杀它们。一些海洋科学家担心，即使是通常对鲸鱼无害的龙涎香贸易，也会为捕鲸提供额外的动机。

G One of the forms ambergris is used today is as a valuable fixative in perfumes to enhance and prolong the scent. But nowadays, since ambergris is rare and expensive, most large fragrance suppliers and manufacturers have realized this and choose not to deal in it because of the cost, availability and complex legal issues. They instead use a chemical derivative which imitates the properties of ambergris. As a fragrance consumer, you can assume that there is no natural ambergris in your perfume bottle, unless the company advertises this fact and unless the bottle was created before the 1980s--and if you own vintage bottles, it may be worth checking.

今天人们对于龙涎香的一种使用方式是在香水中作为一种有价值的固定剂来增强和延长香味。但如今，龙涎香既稀有又昂贵大多数大型香水供应商和制造商已经意识到这一点，由于成本、可用性和复杂的法律问题，他们选择不经营龙涎香。他们转而使用与龙涎香性质相似的化学衍生物。作为一名香水消费者，你可以认为你的香水瓶里没有天然的龙涎香，除非该公司宣传这一事实，除非这款香水是在20世纪80年代之前生产的——如果你拥有老式香水，那么可能值得一查。
