Mystery of Elephant Communication

大象交流的奥秘

A O'Connel-Rodwell, a postdoctoral fellow from Stanford University, came to Namibia's foremost wildlife sanctuary, Etosha, to find out more about the little-known subject of elephant communication. Almost 20 years ago, researchers, including  O'Connel-Rodwell made the surprising discovery that elephants communicate over long distances using extremely low-frequency sounds, also called infrasound, which are generally too deep to be heard by humans.

一位来自斯坦福大学的博士后研究员O'Connell-Rodwell来到纳米比亚最重要的野生动物保护区埃托沙，想要了解更多关于大象交流这一鲜为人知的话题。大约20年前，包括O'Connell-Rodwell在内的研究人员有了一个惊人的发现：大象在长距离的交流中使用的是极低频的声音，也被称为次声，它太低沉以致于人类一般听不到这种声音。

B It is possible to find some clues to the elephant's ability to hear infrasound within the structure of the ear itself. The hammer bone of the elephant's inner ear is proportionally very large, which is typical for other animals that use vibrational signals. Additionally, the elephant and its relative, the manatees, are unusual among mammals for having a cochlear structure in the inner ear which is similar to that of many reptiles. In reptiles, this cochlear structure facilitates keen sensitivity to vibrations, and the same seems to be indicated in elephants.

在大象耳朵本身的结构中，我们可以找到一些线索来了解大象的次声听觉能力。大象内耳的锤状骨比例非常大，这是其他使用振动信号的动物的典型特征。此外，大象和它的近亲海牛在哺乳动物中是不寻常的，因为它们的内耳有一个类似于许多爬行动物的耳蜗结构。在爬行动物中，这种耳蜗结构促进了对振动的敏感，在大象身上似乎也有同样的效果。

C There are other indications in the elephant's anatomy to support their ability to hear infrasound. Firstly, the sheer size of their bodies allows them to generate low-frequency sounds which can be almost as powerful as a jet take-off. Their large bodies also provide ideal 'frames' for receiving vibrations through the ground and up to their heads. Secondly, their toe bones rest on fatty pads which may help to focus vibrations from the ground into the bone. Thirdly, the way the elephant brain lies within the cranial cavity, behind the eyes and in line with the auditory canal, may help them feel the vibrations. The front of the skull has many sinus cavities which might function as resonating chambers for receiving the vibrations from the ground.

在大象的解剖结构中还有其他的迹象支持它们听到次声的能力。第一，它们庞大的体型让它们能够发出低频的声音，几乎和喷气式飞机起飞时一样强大。它们巨大的身体也提供了理想的“框架”来通过地面和头部接收振动。第二，他们的趾骨放在脂肪垫上，这有助于将地面振动集中到骨头上。第三，大象的大脑位于颅腔内，在眼睛后面，与听道一致，这有助于它们感知振动。颅骨的前部有许多窦腔，这些窦腔可以作为接收地面振动的共振腔。

D O'Connel-Rodwell’s background reveals the reason why she was the one to realize that elephants used seismic communication. “I did my master's thesis on seismic communication in planthoppers, ”she wrote. “I'd put a male planthopper on a stem and play back a female call, and the male would do the same thing the elephants were doing：He would freeze, then press down on his legs, go forward a little bit, then freeze again. It was just so fascinating to me, and it's what got me to think, maybe there's something else going on other than acoustic communication. ”It was already known that seismic communication is common in a number of small animals such as spiders, scorpions, frogs, blind mole rats, kangaroo rats, elephant seals and golden moles, but O'Connell-Rodwell was the first to realize that such a large mammal could also use the method.

O'Connell-Rodwell的背景揭示了为什么她是第一个意识到大象使用地面震动通讯的人。“我的硕士论文是关于飞虱的地面震动通讯”，她写道。“我把一只雄飞虱放在一根茎上，回放一只雌虫的叫声，雄虫会做和大象一样的事情：它会一动不动，然后压下它的腿，向前一点，然后再停住一动不动。这对我来说太吸引人了，这让我想到，也许除了声音交流之外，还有其他的东西在起作用。”众所周知，地面震动通讯在许多小动物中很常见，如蜘蛛、蝎子、青蛙、盲鼹鼠、袋鼠、海象和金鼹鼠, 但O'Connell-Rodwell是第一个意识到如此大的哺乳动物也用这种方法。

E Further research has shown that this ability to communicate over long distances is actually essential to the survival of the species. For example, if a predator is detected in the area, experienced members of the herd emit intense warning calls which prompt the herd members to come together for safety and then leave the area. In 1994, O'Connell-Rodwell recorded the warning sounds of a breeding herd being threatened by lions at Mushara. “The elephants got really scared, and the matriarch, the oldest and largest female cow, made these very powerful warning calls, and then the herd took off screaming and trumpeting, ”she recalls, “Since then，every time we played that particular call at the water hole, we get the same response-the elephants take off. ”

进一步的研究表明，这种远距离沟通的能力实际上对这种物种的生存至关重要。例如，如果在该地区发现捕食者，经验丰富的象群成员会发出强烈的警告声，促使象群成员为了安全而聚集在一起，然后离开该地区。1994年, O'Connell-Rodwell记录下了在穆沙拉的一个繁殖象群受到狮子威胁的警告声。“大象很害怕，雌性首领，即最年长体型最大的母象，发出非常强大的警告声，然后象群开始尖叫和吼叫着离开，”她回忆说，“从那以后，每次水坑播放那个声音，我们都会得到同样的反应——大象们离开。”

F In this case, the warning sounds are transmitted through the air, so researchers wanted to find out more about whether elephants could detect calls transmitted through the ground. In 2002，they devised an experiment using electronic equipment that allowed them to send signals through the ground. “The results of our 2002 study showed us that elephants do indeed detect warning calls played through the ground, ”O'Connell-Rodwell observed. “We expected them to clump up into tight groups and leave the areas, and that's in fact what they did. But since we only played back one type of call, we coudn't really say whether they were interpreting it correctly. ”Clearly, further study was needed as the elephants may interpret the sound as something strange.

在这个案例中，警报声音是可以通过空气传播的，所以研究人员想要了解更多关于大象是否能探测到通过地面传播的声音。2002年，他们设计了一项使用电子设备的实验，使他们能够通过地面发送信号。“我们2002年的研究结果显示，大象确实能探测到地面发出的警报, ”O'Connell-Rodwell说道。“我们期望它们会结成紧密的群体，然后离开这个地区，而事实上它们确实这样做了。但是因为我们只回放了一种类型的呼叫，所以我们不能确定它们是否理解正确。”显然，由于大象可能会将这种声音理解为某种奇怪的东西，因此还需要进一步的研究。


G Another experiment in the same area in 2004 was aimed at further studying elephants' sound-detecting abilities by using three different recordings-the 1994 warning call from Mushara, an anti-predator call recorded by scientist Joyce Poole in Kenya, and an artificial warble tone. The recordings were played at random and at various times to see if the elephants would recognize them as distinct vibrations.  O'Connel-Rodwell explained that the aim was to determine whether they can discriminate between familiar and unfamiliar calls that are played back as seismic signals. Even though the analysis phase of the research data from the 2004 experiment is still under way, O'Connell-Rodwell is able to make a few preliminary observations and conclude that the reactions of the elephants were exactly as expected. “When the 1994 warning call was played back, they tended to clump together and leave the water hole sooner. But what's really interesting is that the unfamiliar anti-predator call from Kenya also caused them to clump up, get nervous and aggressively rumble, but they didn't necessarily leave. I didn't think it was going to be that clear-cut. ”

2004年在同一地区进行的另一项实验旨在进一步研究大象的声音探测能力，实验使用了三种不同的录音——1994年穆沙拉的警告信号；科学家乔伊斯·普尔在肯尼亚记录下了一个反捕食者的信号；还有一种人造的颤音。这些录音被随机播放，并在不同的时间播放，以观察大象是否能认出它们是不同的振动。O'Connell-Rodwell解释说, 他们的目的是要确定它们是否能够区分以地面震动信号形式回放的熟悉和不熟悉的呼叫。尽管2004年实验的研究数据仍在分析阶段，O'Connell-Rodwell能够做一些初步的观察和总结，大象的反应就像预期的那样。“当1994警告声被回放时，它们倾向于聚集在一起，尽早离开水坑。但真正有趣的是，来自肯尼亚的陌生的反捕食者的呼唤也导致它们聚集起来，变得紧张并且攻击性地发出隆隆声，但它们不一定离开。我没想到它会这么清晰明确。”
