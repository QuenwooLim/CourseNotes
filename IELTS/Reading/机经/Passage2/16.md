Quantitative Research Versus Qualitative Research in Education

教育中的定量研究与定性研究

A  Jean Piaget was a Swiss developmental psychologist and was the first scientist to undertake experiments based on the theory that children go through four stages of development, 「Q18」 and that only when they have reached the necessary stage of development can they perform the most advanced forms of cognitive operation. One of his famous experiments involved children comparing the amounts of liquid held by containers of different shapes, but of the same volume. Even when shown that the same amount of liquid could be poured into both containers, many of the young subjects would still insist that one container was larger than the other. 「Q19」 「Q16」 Piaget's interpretation of this was that the children were unable to perform the logical task involved in recognizing that the two containers, while different in shape, were the same in capacity. This is because their cognitive development had not reached the necessary stage. Other scientists, however disputed his concl 「Q20」 usion. For instance, 「Q14」 developmental psychologist Margaret Donaldson raised the possibility that the children were simply unwilling to participate in the experimenter's game. 「Q21」 Some critics also point out that these experiments involve social situations in which personal interactions take place, and more could be involved than what Piaget had in mind. Piaget's experiments may not only be measuring the children's capacity for logical thinking-their understanding of the requirements and the abilities of the experimenters to communicate, and the children's willingness to comply with the requirements could make a significant difference to the outcomes.
让·皮亚杰是瑞士发展心理学家，是第一个根据儿童经历四个发展阶段的理论，以及只有当儿童达到必要的发展阶段时他们才能进行最高级的认知操作这一理论进行实验的科学家。他的一个著名的实验是让孩子们比较不同形状但体积相同的容器中液体的量。即使被展示在两个容器中可以倒同样数量的液体时，许多年龄小的受试者仍然坚持认为一个容器比另一个大。皮亚杰对此的解释是，这些孩子们无法执行逻辑任务，即认识到两个容器虽然形状不同，但容量相同这是因为他们的认知发展还没有达到必要的阶段。然而，其他科学家对他的结论提出了质疑。例如，发展心理学家玛格丽特·唐纳森提出了这样一种可能性：孩子们只是不愿意玩实验者的游戏。一些批评家还指出，这些实验涉及人与人的互动的社会情景，比皮亚杰所想的之外的更多东西可能涉及其中。皮亚杰的实验可能不仅仅是测量孩子们的逻辑思维能力——他们对要求的理解和实验者的交流能力，以及孩子们遵守实验要求的意愿可能会对结果产生显著的影响。

B  Comparable criticisms have been made in relation to other psychological and educational tests. 「Q15」 For example, Ed Meehan, a child psychologist, points out how test questions may be interpreted in ways different from those intended by the researcher. In a language development test, children were presented with a picture of a medieval fortress, along with moat, drawbridge, and parapets. Below the picture were three consonants: D, C and G. The children were asked to circle the correct initial consonant for the name of the item in the picture. To select C below the picture of the castle would have been considered correct by the researchers, but many children unexpectedly chose D. When questioned afterwards as to what they thought the picture was of, many children said “Disneyland”. These children used the reasoning which the researchers expected, and yet arrived at the wrong substantive answer. The score sheet showing a wrong answer does not document a child's lack of reasoning ability； it only documents that the child indicated an answer different from the one the tester expected.  

其他心理和教育测试也受到类似的批评。例如，儿童心理学家艾德· 米汉指出测试问题可能会以不同于研究者预期的方式被解释。在一项语言发展测试中孩子们看到了一幅中世纪堡垒的图片，还有护城河、吊桥和防护墙。图片的下面是三个辅音字母：D，C和G。孩子们被要求正确圈出图片中物品名称的首个辅音字母。研究人员认为选城堡图片下面的C是正确的，但许多孩子出乎意料地选了D。后来当被问及他们认为这幅画是什么时，许多孩子说“迪斯尼”。这些孩子使用了研究人员所期望的推理，但却得出了错误的实质性答案。显示错误答案的分数表并不能证明孩子缺乏推理能力；它只记录了孩子的回答与测试者期望的不同。

C  This is an example of the questionable validity of the measurements on which the findings of quantitative research are based. 「Q17」 Some, including for example Donaldson, regard these as technical problems that can be overcome by more experimentation. Other，however，including Meehan，believe them to be more than simply problems of particular experiments or tests, but rather serious threats to the validity of this kind of research on a broader level.

这是定量研究结果所依据的测量方法的有效性有问题的一个例子。包括唐纳森在内的一些人认为，这些技术问题可以通过更多的实验来克服。然而，其他一些人，包括米汉，认为它们不仅仅是特定实验或测试的问题，而是在更广泛的层面上对这类研究有效性的严重威胁。

D  At the same time questions have also been raised about the assumption built into the ‘logic’ of quantitative educational research where causes can be identified by physical and statistical manipulation of variables. Critics suggest that this fails to take account of the very nature of human social life, assuming it to consist of fixed, mechanical causal relationships, whereas in reality life involves complicated processes of interpretation and negotiation that do not necessarily have determinate outcomes. From this point of view, it is not clear that we can understand why people do what they do in terms of the simple sorts of causal relationships on which quantitative research focuses. Our actual social lives, critics suggest, are much more contextually complex and variable.

与此同时，关于定量教育研究的“逻辑” 中的假设的问题也被提了出来，定量教育研究中的原因可以通过对变量的物理和统计操作来确定。批评者认为，这没有考虑到人类社会生活的本质，假设它是由固定的、机械的因果关系组成，而实际上，生活涉及复杂的解释和协商过程，不一定有确定的结果。从这个角度来看，我们还不清楚我们是否能理解就定量研究所关注的简单的因果关系而言，人们所作所为的原因。批评者认为，我们实际的社会生活在语境上要复杂得多，也多变得多。

E  「Q25」 These sorts of criticisms of quantitative educational research have motivated an increasing number of educational researchers to adopt more qualitative approaches in the last thirty or forty years. This group of researchers has generally rejected efforts to measure and control variables either experimentally or statistically. Quantitative research can take various forms which can be loosely termed 'case study', 'participant observation', 'unstructured interview'，'life history', 'discourse analysis' or 'ethnography' etc.

在过去的三四十年中，这些对定量教育研究的批评促使越来越多的教育研究者采用更多的定性方法。这组研究人员通常拒绝通过实验或统计方法来测量和控制变量。定量研究可以采取多种形式，大致可以称为“案例研究”“参与观察”“非结构化访谈”“生活史”“话语分析”或“民族志”等。

F  「Q22-24」 Qualitative research has a strong emphasis on exploring the nature of particular educational phenomena, rather than setting out to test hypotheses about them. Qualitative researchers have a tendency to work with 'unstructured data'：data that have not been coded at the point of collection in terms of a closed set of analytical categories. When engaging in observation，qualitative researchers therefore audio-or video-record what happens or write detailed open-ended field-notes, rather than coding behaviour in terms of a predefined set of categories，as would a quantitative researcher employing 'systematic observation'. Similarly, when interviewing, open-ended questions will be asked rather than questions requiring predefined answers of the kind typical, for example, of postal questionnaires. Qualitative interviews are often deliberately designed to be similar to a casual conversation.

定性研究非常强调探索特定教育现象的本质，而不是着手测试关于这些现象的假设。定性研究者有一种使用“非结构化数 据”的趋向：即在收集时未按照封闭的分析类别集对数据进行编码。因此，在进行观察时，定性研究人员会用音频或视频记录所发生的事情，或撰写详细的开放式实地笔记，而不是像采用“系统观察”的定量研究人员那样，根据预先设定的类别对行为进行编码。同样地，在面谈时，将会提出开放式的问题，而不是需要预先确定答案的问题，例如邮政问卷。定性访谈经常被有意设计成类似于随意交谈的形式。

G  Typically, 「Q22-24」 a small number of cases will be investigated in detail, rather than any attempt being made to cover a large number, as would be the case in most quantitative research, such as systematic observational studies or social surveys. The analysis of the data relies less on quantification and statistical analysis. It actually involves highly accurate interpretations of the meanings and functions of human actions, and mainly in the form of verbal descriptions and explanations.

通常情况下，少量的案例将被详细调查，而不是像大多数定量研究(如系统观察性研究或社会调查)那样试图覆盖大量的案例。对数据的分析较少依赖于量化和统计分析，它实际上涉及对人类行为的意义和功能的高度准确的解释，主要通过语言描述和解释的形式。
