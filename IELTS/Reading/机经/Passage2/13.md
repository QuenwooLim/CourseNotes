Nutrition and Education

营养与教育

A At the Msekeni Primary School in Malawi, children are gathered under the shade of yellow-blossomed acacia trees for their lessons. There are not enough classrooms for every class at the school, but even so, one purpose-built classroom has been emptied and turned into a storeroom for sacks of grain. Here in Malawi, it is clear that food matters more than shelter.

在马拉维的马斯克尼小学，孩子们聚集在黄花刺槐树下上课。学校没有足够的教室供每个班级使用，但即便如此，一间专门建造的教室已经被清空，变成了粮食仓库。在马拉维，粮食显然比住房更重要。

B Malawi is a landlocked southern African country of great beauty and even greater poverty, and Msekeni is one of its poorer regions. Malawians are fortunate not to be experiencing the problems of war or overcrowding or infertile ground, but still they have trouble finding enough food to eat. 「Q20」 Half of the children under five years of age are underfed to the point of stunting their growth. 「Q14」 Hunger affects most aspects of Malawian life, so the country is an excellent subject for research into how nutrition and development are intertwined.
马拉维是一个非洲南部的内陆国家，非常美丽，但很贫穷。马斯克尼是马拉维的贫困地区之一。马拉维人很幸运，没有经历战争、过度拥挤或土地贫瘠的问题，但他们仍然很难找到足够的食物。5岁以下的儿童有一半营养不良，以至于发育不良。饥饿影响着马拉维人生活的方方面面，因此这个国家是研究营养和发展如何交织在一起的绝佳课题。

C Bernard Kumanda is the headmaster at the Msekeni school, and he has strong views on the topic.  K 「Q15」 umanda stated that food is essential to learning, and since 1999 his students have been receiving free school lunches of grain made up of mixed maize and soybean flour, enriched with vitamin A, which is donated through the World Food Program. 「Q21」 Local volunteers do the cooking, turning the dry grain into a bland yet nutritious porridge to be spooned out onto plastic plates. The children eagerly line up for their share, often singing a cheerful song they call “We are getting porridge”.

伯纳德·库曼达是马斯克尼学校的校长，他在这个问题上有深刻的认知。库曼达说，食物对学习至关重要，从1999年开始他的学生就可以免费得到学校午餐，午餐是由玉米和大豆粉混合制成的谷物，富含维生素A，由世界粮食计划署捐赠。当地的志愿者负责做饭，把干粮做成清淡却有营养的粥，然后用勺子舀到塑料盘子里。孩子们急切地排队分粥，经常唱着一首欢快的歌，给这首歌起名为“我们要喝粥了”。

D 「Q22」 「Q16」 Interestingly, the enrolment of students at the Msekeni school doubled when the school's food program was introduced. A number of pupils transferred from other nearby schools，but most new enrolments were children whose families had previously kept them at home to work. These families were so poor that the long-term benefits of educating their children paled against the short-term gain of sending their children out to work in the fields or to gather firewood. The offer of one plate of porridge a day at school completely altered the situation. 「Q23」 On average, when a school in Malawi starts providing free food, there is a 38% rise in the attendance of girls and 24% in boys. A child receiving food at school will not cry so plaintively for food at home. As an extra incentive, girls, who are more likely than boys to be kept at home, are even given extra snacks to take home.

有趣的是，在马斯克尼学校的食品项目推出后，该校的学生人数增加了一倍。许多学生是从附近的其他学校转来的，但大多数新入学的学生是那些家人曾让他们留在家里干活的孩子。这些家庭太穷了，教育孩子的长期利益与送孩子下地干活或拾柴火的短期利益相形见绌。学校每天提供一盘粥完全改变了这种情况。平均来说，当马拉维的一所学校开始提供免费食物时，女生的出勤率上升了38%，男生上升了24%。一个在学校得到食物的孩子在家里就不会这样哭着要食物了。作为一种额外的激励措施，比男孩更有可能被留在家里的女孩子们甚至会得到额外的零食带回家。

E You would expect that a school suddenly taking on significantly more students from poor homes would see a decrease in pass marks, because in most parts of the world, students from poor homes tend to perform worse than those from more prosperous backgrounds. 「Q17」 Combining the influx of students in Msekeni with no increase in teacher numbers, you would expect the pass rates to fall even further. However, in the case at Msekeni, the opposite has proved true. Pass rates have actually improved from 30% to 85%. Although this is a particularly dramaticexample of rises in pass rates, national data shows that the results of school food programs are good overall. While the pass rates for boys tend to remain the same, the rates for girls increase 9.5%. The fact that the effect of feeding is more pronounced in girls than in boys gives a clue to who is eating first in Malawian households-it isn’t the girls.

你可能会认为，一所学校突然招收更多来自贫困家庭的学生，会导致考试分数下降，因为在世界上大多数地方，来自贫困家庭的学生往往比来自富裕家庭的学生表现更差。综合马斯克尼学生的涌入和没有增加的教师人数，你会认为通过率会下降得更低。然而，在马斯克尼的情况恰恰相反。通过率实际上已经从30%提高到85%。虽然这是通过率上升的一个特别引人注目的例子，但全国数据显示，学校食品项目的总体效果很好。虽然男孩的通过率往往保持不变，但女孩的通过率却增长了9.5%。供应食物的效果在女孩身上比在男孩身上更明显，这一事实为马拉维家庭中谁先吃东西提供了线索——不是女孩。

F 「Q18」 「Q27」 Better nutrition generally means brighter children. 「Q24」 Almost immediately, well-fed children find it easier to concentrate on their lessons-it can be well imagined what would happen to a child if his stomach is always crying out for food. “It would be easy to identify children who were critically undernourished. They were the ones who stared into space and didn’t respond when you asked them questions, ”Kumanda says. Even more importantly though, sufficient nutritious food helps young brains grow and develop. 「Q25」 Like all organs, the brain needs both exercise and nutrition. Lacking necessary calories, protein and micronutrients, its growth will be stunted, just like the growth of muscles. This is why feeding children at school makes such a dramatic impact.

更好的营养通常意味着可以培养更聪明的孩子。 「Q24」 吃得好的孩子几乎马上觉得更容易集中精力学习——可以想象如果一个孩子的胃总是饿得咕咕叫，会发生什么事。“很容易确定哪些儿童严重营养不良。他们是眼睛放空而当你问他们问题时不做回答的那些，”库曼达说。而更重要的是，充足的营养食物有助于年轻大脑的生长和发育。像所有的器官一样，大脑需要运动和营养。缺乏必要的热量、蛋白质和微量营养素，它的生长就会受到阻碍，就像肌肉的生长一样。这就是为什么在学校为孩子提供食物会产生如此巨大的影响。

G Three hundred years ago, chronic malnutrition was more or less a worldwide problem. 「Q19」 However, now, on a global scale, people are eating better than ever before. Humans grow to around 50% larger than before the industrial revolution, and malnutrition is extremely rare in rich countries. Even in developing countries, where the majority of Earth's population lives, plates and bowls are fuller than ever before. The percentage of children under the age of five in developing countries who are malnourished fell from 39% in 1990 to 30% in 2000，according to the World Health Organization. In other places too, the fight against hunger appears to be quite successful. Better nutrition has the effect of making people smarter and more energetic, which in turn helps them to grow more prosperous. 「Q26」 And ironically, going from poor to well-off, people suddenly start to worry about becoming obese.

三百年前，慢性营养不良或多或少是一个世界性问题。然而，现在，在全球范围内，人们吃得比以往任何时候都好。人类体型比工业革命前增长了50%左右，营养不良在富裕国家极为罕见。即使在地球上大多数人口居住的发展中国家，盘子和碗也比以往任何时候都要满。根据世界卫生组织的数据，发展中国家5岁以下营养不良的儿童比例已从1990年的39%降至2000年的30%。在其他地方，与饥饿的斗争似乎也相当成功。更好的营养能使人们更聪明、更有活力，从而帮助他们变得更富裕。讽刺的是，从贫穷到富裕，人们突然开始担心变胖。
