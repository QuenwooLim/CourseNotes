Stealth Forces in Weight Loss

减肥的神秘力量
The field of weight loss is like the ancient fable about the blind men and the elephant. Each man investigates a different part of the animal and reports back, only to discover their findings are bafflingly incompatible.
减肥领域就像盲人摸象这个古代寓言故事。 每个人都摸到大象的不同部分并做出报告，但结果发现他们的报告总是令人感到困惑和矛盾。
A     The various findings by public-health experts, physicians, psychologists, geneticists, molecular biologists, and nutritionists are about as similar as an elephant’s tusk is to its tail. 「Q23」 Some say obesity is largely predetermined by our genes and biology; others attribute it to an overabundance of fries, soda, and screen-sucking; still others think we’re fat because of viral infection, insulin, or the metabolic conditions we encountered in the womb. “Everyone subscribes to their own little theory,” 「Q23」 says Robert Berkowitz, medical director of the Center for Weight and Eating Disorders at the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine. We’re programmed to hang onto the fat we have, and some people are predisposed to create and carry more fat than others. Diet and exercise help, but in the end the solution will inevitably be more complicated than pushing away the plate and going for a walk. “It’s not as simple as ‘You’re fat because you’re lazy,’” says Nikhil Dhurandhar, an associate professor at Pennington Biomedical Research Center in Baton Rouge. “Willpower is not a prerogative of thin people. It’s distributed equally.”
A 公共卫生专家、内科医生、心理学家、遗传学家、分子生物学家和营养学家的各种研究结果就像是拿大象象牙与尾巴来做比较。有人说，肥胖主要是由我们的基因和生物学决定；其他人把它归咎于过多的薯条、苏打水和久坐看电视的习惯；还有人认为我们胖是因为病毒感染，胰岛素，或是我们在子宫当中形成的代谢条件。“所有人都坚信自己的一套理论，” 宾夕法尼亚大学医学院体重与饮食失调中心的医学主任罗伯特·伯科维茨说。我们习惯于控制自己的脂肪，有些人则倾向于产生和携带更多的脂肪。节食和锻炼对你有帮助，但最终解决办法不可避免地要比节食和散步更复杂。“这不是简单的‘你胖是因为你懒惰’，”巴吞鲁日Pennington生物医学研究中心的副教授Nikhil Dhurandhar说，“意志力不是瘦人的特权。它是平均分布的。”
B     Science may still be years away from giving us a miracle formula for fat-loss. hormone leptin is a crucial player in the brain’s weight-management circuitry. Some people produce too little leptin; others become desensitized to it. And when obese people lose weight, their leptin levels plummet along with their metabolism. The body becomes more efficient at using fuel and conserving fat, which makes it tough to keep the weight off. 「Q17」 Obese dieters’ bodies go into a state of chronic hunger, a feeling Rudolph Leibel, an obesity researcher at Columbia University, compares to thirst. “Some people might be able to tolerate chronic thirst, but the majority couldn’t stand it,” says Leibel. “Is that a behavioral problem -a lack of willpower? I don’t think so.”
B 科学也许还有几年的时间才能给我们一个神奇的减肥秘方。荷尔蒙瘦素在体重管理脑系统中扮演着关键角色。有些人产生的瘦素太少；有些人则对此并不敏感。当肥胖的人减肥时，他们的瘦素水平随着新陈代谢水平急剧下降。身体在燃料使用和脂肪保存方面变得更有效率，这使得减轻体重变得困难。这时，节食者的身体会进入一种长期的饥饿状态，哥伦比亚大学的肥胖研究人员鲁道夫·雷贝尔(Rudolph Leibel)则认为这是一种渴望。“有些人可能会忍受得了这种长期渴望，但大多数人无法忍受，”Leibel说。“这是一个行为问题——缺乏意志力吗? 我不这么认为。”
C     The government has long espoused moderate daily exercise -of the evening-walk or take-the-stairs variety-but that may not do much to budge the needle on the scale. A 150-pound person burns only 150 calories on a half-hour walk, the equivalent of two apples. It’s good for the heart, less so for the gut. “Radical changes are necessary,” says Deirdre Barrett, a psychologist at Harvard Medical School and author of Waistland. 「Q22」 “People don’t lose weight by choosing the small fries or taking a little walk every other day.” Barrett suggests talking a cue from the members of the National Weight Control Registry (NWCR), 「Q16」 a self-selected group of more than 5,000 successful weight-losers who have shed an average of 66 pounds and kept it off 5.5 years. Some registry members lost weight using low-carb diets; some went low-fat; others eliminated refined foods. Some did it on their own; others relied on counseling. That said, not everyone can lose 66 pounds and not everyone needs to. 「Q21」 The goal shouldn’t be getting thin, but getting healthy. It’s enough to whittle your weight down to the low end of your set range, says Jeffrey Friedman, a geneticist at Rockefeller University. Losing even 10 pounds vastly decreases your risk of diabetes, heart disease, and high blood pressure. The point is to not give up just because you don’t look like a swimsuit model.
C 长期以来，政府一直主张适度的日常锻炼—傍晚散步或爬楼梯——但这种做法可能对减轻体重没有太大作用。一个150磅的人步行半小时会消耗150卡路里，相当于两个苹果的热量。这对心脏有好处，但对内脏来说就没那么好了。“根本的改变是必要的，”哈佛医学院的心理学家、《腰围》一书的作者迪尔德丽·巴雷特(Deirdre Barrett)说。“人们通过选择小份薯条或者每隔一天散步一次的方式来减肥。”Barrett建议从国家体重控制登记处(NWCR)的成员那里得到些许线索。NWCR是一个由5000多名成功减肥者组成的自选团体，他们平均减掉了66磅，并保持了5.5年。一些注册会员使用低碳水化合物饮食减肥;一些选择低脂饮食; 一些人不吃精制食品。有些人依靠个人毅力减肥;其他人则依赖咨询。也就是说，不是每个人都能减掉66磅，也不是每个人都需要。目标不应该是变瘦，而是要变健康。把你的体重降到你设定的范围内就足够了。洛克菲勒大学(Rockefeller University)的遗传学家杰弗里•弗里德曼(Jeffrey Friedman)表示，减重10磅可以大大降低患糖尿病、心脏病和高血压的风险。关键是不要仅仅因为你看起来不像泳装模特就放弃。
D   「Q19」    The negotiation between your genes and the environment begins on day one. Your optimal weight, written by genes, appears to get edited early on by conditions even before birth, inside the womb. 「Q15」 If a woman has high blood-sugar levels while she’s pregnant, her children are more likely to be overweight or obese, according to a study of almost 10,000 mother-child pairs. 「Q20」 Maternal diabetes may influence a child’s obesity risk through a process called metabolic imprinting, says Teresa Hillier, an endocrinologist with Kaiser Permanente’s Center for Health Research and the study’s lead author. The implication is clear: Weight may be established very early on, and obesity largely passed from mother to child. Numerous studies in both animals and humans have shown that a mother’s obesity directly increases her child’s risk for weight gain. The best advice for moms-to-be: Get fit before you get pregnant. You’ll reduce your risk of complications during pregnancy and increase your chances of having a normal-weight child.
D 你的基因和环境之间的较量从第一天就开始了。你最理想的体重由基因决定，它似乎是在出生之前在子宫内就已经开始被编辑了。一项针对近万对母子的研究表明，如果女性在怀孕期间血糖水平较高，那么她的孩子更可能会超重或肥胖。Kaiser Permanente健康研究中心的内分泌学家、该研究的主要作者Teresa Hillier说，母亲的糖尿病可能会通过一种叫做新陈代谢的过程来影响儿童的肥胖风险。其含义很明确: 体重可能在很早的时候就确定了，而肥胖在很大程度上是由母亲传给孩子的。大量的动物和人类的研究表明，母亲的肥胖直接增加了孩子体重增加的风险。对准妈妈们最好的建议是：在怀孕前要保持健康身材。在怀孕期间，你会减少并发症的风险，而且拥有正常体重孩子的机会也会大大增加。
E     It’s the $64,000 question: Which diets work? It got people wondering: Isn’t there a better way to diet? A study seemed to offer an answer. 「Q14」 The paper compared two groups of adults: those who, after eating, secreted high levels of insulin, a hormone that sweeps blood sugar out of the bloodstream and promotes its storage as fat, and those who secreted less. Within each group, half were put on a low-fat diet and half on a low-glycemic-load diet. On average, the low-insulin-secreting group fared the same on both diets, losing nearly 10 pounds in the first six months – but they gained about half of it back by the end of the 18-month study. The high-insulin group didn’t do as well on the low-fat plan, losing about 4.5 pounds, and gaining back more than half by the end. But the most successful were the high-insulin-secretors on the low-glycemic-load diet. They lost nearly 13 pounds and kept it off.
E 这是一个价值64,000美元的问题: 哪种节食法有效?这让人们感到疑惑:难道没有更好的饮食方式吗?一项研究似乎给出了答案。这篇论文比较了两组成年人：一组饮食后分泌出了高水平的胰岛素（即能将血糖从血液中清除并将其储存为脂肪的激素），而另外一组则分泌较少。在每组中，一半人进行低脂饮食，一半人进行低糖饮食。平均而言，低胰岛素分泌组在这两种饮食上都是一样的，在头六个月里减掉了将近10磅，但在18个月的研究结束后，他们又恢复了大约一半的体重。高胰岛素组在低脂肪饮食计划上的效果并不好，减掉了4.5磅，到最后又反弹了一半以上。但最成功的是低血糖负荷饮食中的高胰岛素分泌者。他们减掉了近13磅，并保持了下来。
F     What if your fat is caused not by diet or genes, but by germs-say, a virus? It sounds like a sci-fi horror movie, but research suggests some dimension of the obesity epidemic may be attributable to infection by common viruses, says Dhurandhar. The idea of “infectobesity” came to him 20 years ago when he was a young doctor treating obesity in Bombay. 「Q24」 He discovered that a local avian virus, SMAM-1, caused chickens to die, sickened with organ damage but also, strangely, with lots of abdominal fat. In experiments, Dhurandhar found that SMAM-1 infected chickens became obese on the same diet as uninfected ones, which stayed svelte.
F 如果你的肥胖不是由饮食或基因引起的，而是由细菌，比如病毒引起的，那该怎么办呢？这听起来像一个科幻恐怖电影，但研究表明，有些肥胖可能是由于常见的病毒感染引起的，Dhurandhar说。20年前，当他还是个年轻的医生在孟买治疗肥胖症时，他想到了“感染性肥胖症”这个概念。他发现当地的一种禽流感病毒SMAM-1会导致鸡的死亡和器官受损，但奇怪的是，死亡的鸡有大量的腹部脂肪。在实验中，Dhurandhar发现SMAM-1感染的鸡在相同的饮食中变得肥胖，而未受感染的鸡则保持苗条。
G     「Q25」 He later moved to the U.S and onto a bona fide human virus, adenovirus 36 (AD-36). In the lab, every species of animal Dhurandhar infected with the virus became obese-chickens got fat, mice got fat, even rhesus monkeys at the zoo that picked up the virus from the environment suddenly gained 15 percent of their body weight upon exposure. 「Q18」 In his latest studies, 「Q26」 Dhurandhar has isolated a gene that, when blocked from expressing itself, seems to turn off the virus’s fattening power. Stem cells extracted from fat cells and then exposed to AD-36 reliably blossom into fat cells-but when stem cells are exposed to an AD-36 virus with the key gene inhibited, the stems cells don’t differentiate. The gene appears to be necessary and sufficient to trigger AD-36-related obesity, 「Q27」 and the goal is to use the research to create a sort of obesity vaccine.
G 后来他搬到了美国，开始研究一种真正的人类病毒——腺病毒36 (AD-36)。在实验室里，每一种被病毒感染的动物都变胖了——鸡变胖了，老鼠变胖了，甚至在动物园里突然感染病毒的恒河猴也突然增加了15%的体重。在他的最新研究中，Dhurandhar已经分离出一种基因，当它被阻止表达时，它似乎会抑制病毒的增肥能力。从脂肪细胞中提取干细胞，然后暴露在AD-36中，干细胞就会分化成脂肪细胞，但是当干细胞暴露在关键基因被抑制的AD-36病毒中时，干细胞就不会分化成脂肪细胞。该基因似乎是触发与AD-36相关的肥胖症的必要和充分条件，他的目的就是利用该研究来研制一种肥胖症疫苗。
