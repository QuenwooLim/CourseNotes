Is Graffiti Art or Crime?

涂鸦是艺术还是犯罪？
A  The term graffiti derives from the Italian graffio meaning ‘scratching’ and can be defined as uninvited markings or writing scratched or applied to objects, built structures and natural features. It is not a new phenomenon: examples can be found on ancient structures around the world, in some cases predating the Greeks and Romans. In such circumstances 「Q24」 it has acquired invaluable historical and archaeological significance, providing a social history of life and events at that time. Graffiti is now a problem that has become pervasive, as a result of the availability of cheap and quick means of mark-making.

A  涂鸦这个词源于意大利语“graffio”，意思是“乱画”，它可以被定义为不请自来的标记，或者是对物体、建筑结构和自然特征的涂画。这并不是一种新现象: 世界各地的古代建筑中都可以找到这样的例子，有一些甚至比古希腊古罗马还早。在这种情况下，涂鸦记录了当时的社会历史和事件，因此具有宝贵的历史和考古意义。如今由于涂画方式成本廉价，方式快捷，涂鸦成为一个普遍的难题。
B 「Q19」 It is usually considered a priority to remove graffiti as quickly as possible after it appears. This is for several reasons. The first is to prevent ‘copy-cat’ emulation which can occur rapidly once a clean surface is defaced. It may also be of a racist or otherwise offensive nature and many companies and councils have a policy of removing this type of graffiti within an hour or two of it being reported. Also, as paints, glues and inks dry out over time they can become increasingly difficult to remove and are usually best dealt with as soon as possible after the incident. 「Q16」 Graffiti can also lead to more serious forms of vandalism and, ultimately, the deterioration of an area, contributing to social decline.

B 出现涂鸦之后，人们通常会把尽快移除涂鸦作为优先选择，这有以下几个原因。第一个原因就是防止“模仿者”盲目的跟随，一旦一块干净的地方被涂画了之后，其他地方不久就会出现类似情况。也有可能是涂鸦本身带有种族主义或攻击性，许多公司和理事会制订了在事件报道的一至两个小时内清除此类涂鸦的政策。而且，随着时间的推移，颜料、胶水和墨水会逐渐变干，它们会变得越来越难以去除，因此通常最好在事件发生后尽快处理。涂鸦还会引起更严重的破坏行为，最终导致一个地区的恶化，社会也随之衰落。
C Although graffiti may be regarded as an eyesore, any proposal to remove it from sensitive historic surfaces should be carefully considered: techniques designed for more robust or utilitarian surfaces may result in considerable damage. In the event of graffiti incidents, i 「Q18」 t is important that the owners of buildings or other structures and their consultants are aware of the approach they should take in dealing with the problem. The police should be informed as there may be other related attacks occurring locally. 「Q25」 An incidence pattern can identify possible culprits, as can stylised signatures or nicknames, known as tags, which may already be familiar to local police. Photographs are useful to record graffiti incidents and may assist the police in bringing a prosecution. Such images are also required for insurance claims, and can be helpful to cleaning operatives, allowing them to see the problem area before arriving on site.

C 尽管涂鸦可能被认为是一种难看的东西，但任何以敏感的历史角度考虑并将其移除的建议都应该被仔细考虑: 更坚固或实用的表面设计的技术可能会造成相当大的破坏。在涂鸦事件发生时，建筑物或其他建筑物的业主和其顾问应意识到采取何种方法来处理这个问题。当地可能还会发生其它类似事件，所以应该及时通知警察。相关的涂鸦事件可以识别可能的罪魁祸首，因为当地警察可能已经熟悉其签名风格或昵称，即所谓的“标签”。照片有助于记录涂鸦事件，并可能协助警方将其绳之以法。这样的图像也需要保险索赔，并且可以帮助清洁人员，让他们在到达现场之前看到问题区域。
D 「Q20-21」 There are a variety of methods that are used to remove graffiti. Broadly these divide between chemical and mechanical systems. 「Q14」 Chemical preparations are based on dissolving the media; these solvents can range from water to potentially hazardous chemical ‘cocktails’. Mechanical systems such as wire-brushing and grit-blasting attempt to abrade or chip the media from the surface. Care should be taken to comply with health and safety legislation with regard to the protection of both passers-by and any person carrying out the cleaning. 「Q26」 Operatives should follow product guidelines in terms of application and removal, and wear the appropriate protective equipment. Measures must be taken to ensure that run-off, aerial mists, drips and splashes do not threaten unprotected members of the public. 「Q14」 When examining a graffiti incident it is important to assess the ability of the substrate to withstand the prescribed treatment. If there is any doubt regarding this, then small trial areas should be undertaken to assess the impact of more extensive treatment.

D 有各种各样的方法用来清除涂鸦。大体上，可分为化学方法和机械方法。化学制剂的工作原理是溶解涂鸦媒介。这些溶剂种类多样，有水也有潜在危险的化学“鸡尾酒”。机械方法如钢丝擦拭或是喷砂，其工作原理都是尝试清除污迹或是将其剥离表面。在操作过程中，应注意要遵守健康和安全法则，保护过往行人和清洁工人。操作人员应遵循产品的运用和清除指南，并穿戴适当的防护设备。在其过程中，必须采取适当措施，确保其施工过程如径流，空中喷雾，水滴和飞溅物不会威胁到未受保护的公众。在检测涂鸦事件时，至关重要的是要去评定涂鸦表面基材是否能承受得住相关的清除方式。如对此有任何疑问，那么应该进行小范围的试验，然后再去评估广泛清除方法的影响。
E A variety of preventive strategies can be adopted to combat a recurring problem of graffiti at a given site. 「Q17」 As no two sites are the same, no one set of protection measures will be suitable for all situations. Each site must be looked at individually. Surveillance systems such as closed circuit television may also help. 「Q22-23」 In cities and towns around the country, prominently placed cameras have been shown to reduce anti-social behaviour of all types including graffiti. Security patrols will also act as a deterrent to prevent recurring attacks. However, the cost of this may be too high for most situations. Physical barriers such as a wall, railings, doors or gates can be introduced to discourage unauthorised access to a vulnerable site. However, consideration has to be given to the impact of the measures that may have on the structure being protected. In the worst cases, they can be almost as damaging to the quality of the environment as the graffiti they prevent. In others, they might simply provide a new surface for graffiti.

F One of the most significant problems associated with graffiti removal is the need to remove it from surfaces that are repeatedly attacked. Under these circumstances the repeated removal of graffiti using even the gentlest methods will ultimately cause damage to the surface material. There may be situations where the preventive strategies mentioned above do not work or are not a viable proposition at a given site. Anti-graffiti coatings are usually applied by brush or spray leaving a thin veneer that essentially serves to isolate the graffiti from the surface.

F 去除涂鸦遇到的最重要的一个问题是需要将其从反复受到侵袭的建筑表面清除。在这种情况之下，涂鸦清除即使使用最温和的清洁方法最终也会伤害其建筑物表面。在某些情况下，上面提到的预防性策略在某一特定地点可能不起作用或者行不通。反涂鸦表层通常是运用刷子或者喷雾形成一层薄的保护膜，这样就可以从根本上隔离建筑物表面的涂鸦。
