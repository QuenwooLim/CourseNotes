The Farmers：the Past and the Present

Farmers的过去和现在

A The Farmers trading company began in 1909 when Scottish immigrant Robert Laidlaw established a mail-order company called Laidlaw Leeds in Fort Street Auckland. He then opened branches via the purchase of the Green & Colebrook chain store, and further regional stores in Auckland and Waikato followed. Then their first furniture and boot factory was opened, and by 1920 the company had 29 branches. A store in Whangarei was purchased, and Hobson Street was opened for direct selling to the public. The firm then went international, establishing London and New York purchasing offices. With permission from the local harbor board， a large Farmers electric sign on the Wyndham Steet front was installed.

Farmers百货开始于1909年。那年苏格兰移民罗伯特·莱德劳在奥克兰的福特街建立了一家名为“莱德劳利兹”的邮购公司随后， 他通过收购Green&Colebrook连锁店开设了分店，随后在奥克兰和怀卡托开设了更多的地区分店。后来，他们的第一家家具和皮靴厂开张了，到1920年，这家公司已经有了29家分公司。在旺加雷的一家商店被收购，霍布森街向公众开放了直销。随后，该公司走向国际，在伦敦和纽约设立了采购办公室。在当地港务局的许可下，一个大的Farmers电牌标志被安装在温德姆街前。

B 「Q17」 It seems that Robert Laidlaw was ahead of his time as an entrepreneur. He might have been scripting a modern-day commercial when he said “Satisfaction, or your money back”.  Business historian Ian Hunter commented, 「Q20」 “It was the first money back guarantee ever offered in New Zealand by any firm, and this statement was only the second one ever found in the world. ”Laidlaw's mission was simple—to build the greatest business in New Zealand，to simplify every transaction, to eliminate all delays, and to only sell goods that are practical for customers.

罗伯特·莱德劳作为企业家似乎领先于时代。当他说“要么满意，要么把钱退给你”时，他可能是在为现代的一个商业广告写剧本。商业历史学家伊恩·亨特评论道：这是新西兰历史上第一家提供退款保证的公司，而该宣言是世界上第二个出现的。莱德劳的使命很简单——建立新西兰最大的企业，简化每一笔交易，消除所有的延误，只销售对客户来说实用的产品。

C 「Q21」 「Q16」 This company, which had humble beginnings as a mail-order business, now employs 3,500 staff in 58 stores, and has just celebrated its hundredth anniversary. I 「Q25」 an Hunter, who is also the author of a history book, says that coming to Farmers every week is part of the New Zealand way of life, and in the 1960s, one in ten people had an account with Farmers. By that time, Farmers was the place for girls to buy their first bra, newlyweds to purchase their first diner-sets， new workers to use their first pay-cheques to pay off hire-purchase furniture，and the best place to see Santa every Christmas. At one time it had tearooms, children's playground, and an annual celebration of the birthday of Hector the Parrot who died in the 1970s aged 131. 「Q22」 At one time, you could even buy houses from Farmers. There was also a saddle factory which supplied the armed forces, and its upright grand pianos offered good value， according to early catalogues.
这家公司最初只是一家不起眼的邮购公司，现在已经在58家店雇佣了3500名员工，并且刚刚庆祝了它的100周年纪念日。也是一本历史书的作者伊恩·亨特说，每周拜访Farmers是新西兰生活方式的一部分， 在20世纪60年代， 十分之一的人都有Farmers的账户。在那个时候， Farmers是女孩们购买第一个胸罩的地方，是新婚夫妇购买第一套餐具的地方，是新工人们用他们的第一笔工资分期付款购买家具的地方，是每年圣诞节看圣诞老人的最佳地点。这里曾经有茶室、儿童游乐场，还有每年一度的鹦鹉赫克托的生日庆祝活动(它在20世纪70年代以131岁高龄去世)。过去，你甚至可以从Farmers那里买房子。根据早期的目录，它还有一家为军队提供马鞍的工厂，其立式大钢琴也物有所值。

D 「Q15」 Farmers has changed considerably from that time. Walking through a Farmers store today you will be drawn by the bright lights and big brands. Its Albany branch hosts 16 international cosmetics companies. The store purchases from about 500 suppliers-with about 30 of those being locally owned. “10 years ago, ”says current CEO Rod McDermot, “lots of brands wouldn't partner with us. The stores were quite distressed. We were first price point focused；we weren’t fashion focused. ”Farmers is, quite simply, a company that does business in hard times with no room for sentimentality. For example, regarding the occasion when Dancing with the Stars presenter Candy Lane launched a clothing line, McDermott commented, “We put a trial on, and we thought it was really lovely, but the uptake wasn't what we thought it would be.  It's got to be what the customer wants. ”

从那时起， Farmers发生了很大的变化。今天， 当你走过一家Farmers店时， 你会被明亮的灯光和大品牌所吸引。其奥尔巴尼分公司拥有16家国际化妆品公司的柜台。这家店从大约500家供应商那里进货，其中大约30家是本地供应商。现任首席执行官罗德·麦克德莫特表示：“10年前，许多品牌不会与我们合作。商店都很忧虑。我们当时首先关注价格，我们不关注时尚。”很简单， Farmers是一家在困难时期做生意的公司，没有多愁善感的余地。例如，当《与星共舞》的主持人坎迪·莱恩推出了一个服装系列时，麦克德莫特评论道：“我们进行了试验，我们认为它真的很好，但接受度并不是我们想象的那样。它必须是客户想要的。”

E 「Q19」 McDermott also acknowledges the achievement of Farmers in making one-hundred years，because retailers are often badly hit when the economy turns down. Farmers almost didn't survive one economic crisis in the mid-1980s, by which time it had stores across the country. It had purchased the South Island's Calder Mackay chain of stores, and bought out Haywrights. 「Q26」 With sales topping $375 million, it was purchased by the Chase Corporation.  Lincoln Laidlaw, son of the founder Robert, recalls the dark days of the stock market crash and the collapse of Chase：“I think, once, Farmers was like a big family and all of the people who worked for it felt they were building something that would ultimately be to their benefit and to the benefit of New Zealand. . . then the business was being divided up and so that kind of family situation was dispelled and it hasn't been recovered. ”During this turbulent period, the stores were controlled by a variety of Australian banks, followed by Deka, the Maori Development Corporation and Foodland Associated Ltd. Finally, in 2003 the ownership changed to family-style after it was purchased by the James Pascoe Group, owned by David and Anne Norman.  

麦克德莫特也认可了Farmers在创造百年历史方面取得的成就，因为当经济衰退时，零售商往往会受到严重的打击。在20世纪80年代中期的一次经济危机中， Farmers几乎没有生存下来，那时它在全国各地都有店。它收购了南岛的Calder Mackay连锁商店， 又买下了Hay wrights。它的销售额高达3.75亿新西兰元， 被Chase Corporation收购。创始人罗伯特的儿子林肯·莱德劳回忆股市崩盘和Chase暴跌的黑暗时期：“我认为， 曾经Farmers就像一个大家庭， 所有为它工作的人都感觉他们在建造某种最终会使他们以及新西兰受益的东西……后来公司被分拆，这种家庭状况被打破了，一直没有恢复。”在这段动荡时期，店由澳大利亚多家银行控制， 接着是Deka、毛利发展公司和Foodland联合有限公司。最后， 在2003年，所有权被大卫和安妮·诺曼拥有的James Pascoe集团收购后， 转变为家庭式的。

F 「Q18」 McDermott attributes the survival of the company through difficult times to its power of brand, and commented that, “ 「Q24」 We're becoming the brand it used to be again. ”Why was the brand so powerful? During World War II, Farmers topped up the wages of any staff member disadvantaed by overseas service. Laidlaw was a committed Christian, and he concluded his original mission statement with the powerful words：“All at it, always at it, wins success." Around 58 stores across New Zealand have announced the local charities they have selected to raise funds for during their centenary celebration―it is likely to include everything from guide-dog services to hospices and volunteer fire brigades. Every dollar raised by the community will be matched by the company. “It’s like the rebirth of an icon,"says McDermott.

麦克德莫特将公司度过困难时期归因于它的品牌力量。“我们正再次成为过去的品牌。”为什么这个品牌如此强大?在第二次世界大战期间，Farmers为任何因海外服务而处于不利地位的工作人员涨工资。莱德劳是一名虔诚的基督徒，他在总结自己最初的使命宣言时用了一句有力的话：“全力以赴永远努力，才能获得成功。新西兰全国约有58家店公布了它们选定在一百周年庆典期间为之筹集资金的当地慈善机构，其中可能包括从导盲犬服务到临终关怀和志愿消防队等各种服务。社区筹集的每一新西兰元都将由公司来匹配。 “这就像一个偶像的重生，” 麦克德莫特说。
