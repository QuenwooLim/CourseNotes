Motivating Drives

激励驱动力

Scientists have been researching the way to get employees motivated for many years. This research is a relational study which builds the fundamental and comprehensive model for study. This is especially true when the business goal is to turn unmotivated teams into productive ones. But their research has limitations. It is like studying the movements of a car without taking out the engine.

科学家们多年来一直在研究激励员工的方法。这项研究是一个关系性研究，建立了基础而全面的研究模型。当商业目标是让缺乏动力的团队变成高效的团队时，这一点尤其正确。但他们的研究有局限性。这就像在不拆开发动机的情况下研究汽车的运转。

Motivation is what drives people to succeed and plays a vital role in enhancing organizational development. It is important to study the motivation of employees because it is related to the emotion and behaviour of employees. Recent studies show there are four drives for motivation. They are the drive to acquire, the drive to bond, the drive to comprehend and the drive to defend.

激励是驱使人们成功的东西，并在促进组织发展方面起着至关重要的作用。研究员工的激励很重要，因为它与员工的情绪和行为有关。最近的研究表明 有四种驱动力：获取驱动力、联结驱动力、理解驱动力和防御驱动力。

The Drive to Acquire

获取驱动力

The drive to acquire must be met to optimise the acquisition aspect as well as the achievement element. Thus the way that outstanding performance is recognised, the type of perks that are provided to polish the career path. But sometimes a written letter of appreciation generates more motivation than a thousand-dollar check, which can serve as the invisible power to boost business engagement. Successful organisations and leaders not only need to focus on the optimization of physical reward but also on moving other levers within the organization that can drive motivation.

获取驱动力必须得到满足，才能优化获取方面以及成就元素。因此，优秀表现被认可的方式、提供来完善职业道路的额外福利类型都很重要。但有时一封书面的感谢信比一张千元支票更能产生激励作用，它可以作为推动业务参与的隐形力量。成功的组织和领导者不仅需要关注物质奖励的优化，还需要关注推动组织内部能够驱动动力的其他杠杆。

The Drive to Bond

联结驱动力

The drive to bond is also key to driving motivation. There are many kinds of bonds between people, like friendship and family ties. In company, employees also want to be an essential part of the company. They want to belong to the company. Employees will be motivated if they find personal belonging to the company. In the meantime, the greatest commitment will be achieved by the employee on condition that the force of motivation within the employee affects the direction, intensity and persistence of decision and behaviour in company.

联结驱动力也是驱动动力的关键。人与人之间有多种联结，比如友谊、家庭。在公司里，员工也想成为公司的重要组成部分。他们想归属于公司。如果员工发现自己在公司有归属感，他们就会受到激励。同时，员工只有在内在动机影响其在公司中的决策和行为的方向、强度和持久性时，才会实现最大的投入度。

The Drive to Comprehend

理解驱动力

The drive to comprehend motivates many employees to higher performance. For years, it has been known that setting stretch goals can greatly impact performance. Organisations need to ensure that the various job roles provide employees with simulation that challenges them or allow them to grow. Employees don’t want to do meaningless things or monotonous jobs. If the job doesn’t provide them with personal meaning and fulfilment, they will leave the company.

理解驱动力激励许多员工达到更高的绩效。多年来，人们已经知道设定延伸目标可以极大地影响绩效。组织需要确保不同的工作岗位能为员工提供具有挑战性或促进成长的激励‌。员工不想做无意义的事情或单调的工作。如果工作没有给他们提供个人意义和成就感，他们会离开公司。

The Drive to Defend

防御驱动力

The drive to defend is often the hardest lever to pull. This drive manifests itself as a quest to create and promote justice, fairness, and the ability to express themselves freely. The organisational level for this basic human motivator is resource allocation. This drive is also met through an employee feeling connection to a company. If their companies are merged with another, they will show worries.

防御驱动力通常是最难拉动的杠杆。这种驱动力表现为寻求创造和促进正义、公平以及自由表达我们自己的能力。在组织层面，这一基本人类动机体现在资源分配上。这种驱动力也通过员工与公司的联系感得到满足。如果他们的公司与另一家公司合并，他们会表现出担忧。

Two studies have been done to find the relations between the four drives and motivation. The article based on two studies was finally published in Harvard Business Review. Most authors’ arguments have laid emphasis on four-drive theory and actual investigations. Using the results of the surveys which executed with employees from Fortune 500 companies and two other global businesses (P company and H company), the article mentions how independent drives influence employees’ behaviour and how organisational levels boost employee motivation.

已经进行了两项研究来找出四种驱动力与激励之间的关系。基于这两项研究的文章最终发表在《哈佛商业评论》上。大多数作者的观点都强调四驱动力理论和实际调查。利用对财富500强公司以及其他两家全球性公司（P公司和H公司）员工进行的调查结果，这篇文章提到独立的驱动力如何影响员工行为以及组织层面如何提高员工积极性。

The studies show that the drive to bond is most related to fulfilling commitment, while the drive to comprehend is most related to how much effect employees spend on work. The drive to acquire can be satisfied by a rewarding system which ties rewards to performance, and gives the best people opportunities for advancement. For the drive to defend, a study on the merging of P company and H company shows that employees in the former company show an unusual cooperating attitude.

研究表明，联结驱动力与实现承诺最相关，而理解驱动力与员工在工作上投入多少努力最相关。获取驱动力可以通过奖励制度来满足，该制度将奖励与绩效挂钩，并为最优秀的人提供晋升机会。对于防御驱动力，一项关于P公司和H公司合并的研究表明，前一家公司的员工表现出异常的合作态度。

The key to successfully motivate employees is to meet all drives. Each of these drives is important if we are to understand employee motivation. These four drives, while not necessarily the only human drives, are the ones that are central to the unified understanding of modern human life.

成功激励员工的关键是满足所有驱动力。如果我们想理解员工激励，这些驱动力中的每一个都很重要。这四种驱动力虽然未必是人类仅有的驱动力，但却是理解现代人类生活的核心所在。
