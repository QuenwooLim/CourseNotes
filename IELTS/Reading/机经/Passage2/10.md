Computer Provides More Questions Than Answers

计算机产生更多的问题，而不是解决问题
A     The island of Antikythera lies 18 miles north of Crete, where the Aegean Sea meets the Mediterranean. Currents there can make shipping treacherous – and one ship bound for ancient Rome never made it. 「Q19」 The ship that sank there was a giant cargo vessel measuring nearly 500 feet long. 「Q17」 It came to rest about 200 feet below the surface, where it stayed for more than 2,000 years 「Q19」 until divers looking for sponges discovered the wreck a little more than a century ago.
A 安提凯希拉岛位于克里特岛以北18英里处，是爱琴海与地中海相接的地方。洋流使得航行很不安全——开往古罗马的船从未成功抵达过。沉没的船舶中有一个近500英尺长的巨型货轮。它在海平面约200英尺以下的地方，在那里一停就是两千多年，直到寻找海绵的潜水员在一个多世纪前发现了它。

Nine months later, 「Q21」 an enterprising archaeologist cleared off a layer of organic material from one of the pieces of junk and found that it looked like a gearwheel. It had inscriptions in Greek characters and seemed to have something to do with astronomy.
B 船体内部有青铜和大理石雕像。从船的外观看，船似乎携带了豪华的物品，这些物品可能是在希腊不同的岛屿上制造的，然后卖给来自不断强大的罗马帝国的富裕顾客。雕像以及其他很多不重要的东西都被修复了，然后被存储了起来。九个月后，一个积极进取的考古学家从一个看起来像齿轮的垃圾物品上清除了一层有机材料。上面有希腊字符式的铭文，似乎与天文学有关。
C     「Q16」 That piece of “junk” went on to become the most celebrated finding from the shipwreck; it is displayed at the National Archaeological Museum of Athens. Research has shown that the wheel was part of a device so sophisticated that its complexity would not be matched for a thousand years – 「Q22」 it was also the world’s first known analog computer. The device is so famous that an international conference organized in Athens a couple of weeks ago had only one subject: the Antikythera Mechanism.
C 那件“垃圾”后来成为沉船发现中最著名的东西，目前展示在雅典国家考古博物馆。有研究表明，车轮是设备的一部分，它是如此的复杂以至于让人怀疑一千年前是否存在这样的设备，这也是世界上第一个已知的模拟计算机。该设备是如此著名，以至于几个星期前在雅典举办的国际会议中只有一个主题：安提凯希拉机器。
D     Every discovery about the device has raised new questions. Who built the device, and for what purpose? Why did the technology behind it disappear for the next thousand years? What does the device tell us about ancient Greek culture? And does the marvelous construction, and the precise knowledge of the movement of the sun and moon and Earth that it implies, tell us how the ancients grappled with ideas about determinism and human destiny?
D 有关设备的每一个发现都提出了新的问题。是谁建的这个设备，目的是什么？为什么它背后的技术在未来的数千年内消失？这个设备会告诉我们关于古希腊文化的什么呢？这个奇妙的装置，以及它所暗示的有关太阳，月亮和地球运动的精确知识，会不会告诉我们古人是如何理解决定论和人类命运的？
E     “ 「Q23」 We have gear trains from the 9th century in Baghdad used for simpler displays of the solar and lunar motions relative to one another – they use eight gears,” said Francois Charette, a historian of science in Germany who wrote an editorial accompanying a new study of the mechanism two weeks ago in the journal Nature.” 「Q23」 In this case, we have more than 30 gears. To see it on a computer animation makes it mind-boggling. 「Q23」 There is no doubt it was a technological masterpiece.”
E“我们有9世纪的来自巴格达的齿轮火车，它用于简单地显示太阳和月球之间的相对运动——他们使用8个齿轮，”Francois Charette说道，他是德国一位科学历史学家，两个星期前，他在Nature杂志上刚刚发表这个新的研究。“这次，我们有超过30个齿轮，要在计算机上看到它的动画，这让人难以置信。毫无疑问，这是一个技术性的杰作。”
F     The device was probably built between 100 and 140 BC, and the understanding of astronomy it displays seems to have been based on knowledge developed by the Babylonians around 300-700 BC, said 「Q25」 Mike Edmunds, a professor of astrophysics at Cardiff University in Britain. He led a research team that reconstructed what the gear mechanism would have looked like by using advanced three-dimensional-imaging technology. The group also decoded a number of the inscriptions. 「Q25」 The mechanism explores the relationship between lunar months – the time it takes for the moon to cycle through its phases, say, full moon to full moon – and calendar years. The gears had to be cut precisely to reflect this complex relationship; 19 calendar years equal 235 lunar months.
F 该设备可能建于公元前100至140年之间，它对天文学的认识似乎是基于公元前300-700年巴比伦人的知识基础之上的，英国卡迪夫大学天体物理学教授迈克·埃德蒙兹说。他领导的一个研究小组将通过使用先进的三维成像技木重建齿轮机构原貌。该小组还解码了一些铭文。这个装置探索了农历月份间的关系，农历时间就是月亮依赖其节奏循环的时间，例如全月到全月以及日历年的时间。齿轮需要切割得十分精确来反映出复杂关系：19个日历年等于235个农历月。
G     By turning the gear mechanism, 「Q25」 which included what Edmunds called a beautiful system of epicyclic gears that factored in the elliptical orbit of the moon, a person could check what the sky would have looked like on a date in the past, or how it would appear in the future. 「Q18」 The mechanism was encased in a box with doors in front and back covered with inscriptions – a sort of instruction manual. Inside the front door were pointers indicating the date and the position of the sun, moon and zodiac, while opening the back door revealed the relationship between calendar years and lunar months, and a mechanism to predict eclipses.
G 通过转动齿轮机构，其中Edmunds称为漂亮行星系统的齿轮装置也在月球椭圆轨道中被考虑进去了，人可以通过观察天空来推测一个日期在过去的状况，以及它会如何出现在未来。该机制是装在一个盒子里，在前面和背面的盖上覆盖有铭文——相当于一种说明书。前门内侧的指针指示日期以及太阳，月亮和生肖的位置，打开后门则显示日历年和阴历月的关系以及预测日食的机制。
H     「Q26」 “If they needed to know when eclipses would occur, and this related to the rising and setting of stars and related them to dates and religious experiences, the mechanism would directly help,” said Yanis Bitsakis, a physicist at the University of Athens who co-wrote the Nature paper. “It is a mechanical computer. You turn the handle and you have a date on the front.” 「Q15」 Building it would have been expensive and required the interaction of astronomers, engineers, intellectuals and craftspeople. Charette said the device overturned conventional ideas that the ancient Greeks were primarily ivory tower thinkers who did not deign to muddy their hands with technical stuff. It is a reminder, he said, that while the study of history often focuses on written texts, they can tell us only a fraction of what went on at a particular time.
H“如果他们需要知道什么时候发生日食，这与星体上升和设置有关，把它们与日期和宗教经验联系起来，这个装置会直接提供帮助，”雅典大学的物理学家Yanis Bitsakis说，他也在Nature中共同撰写了这篇文章。“这是一个机械式的计算机。你转动手柄，它前面就显示日期。”当时构建它会很昂贵，而且需要天文学家，工程师，知识分子和手工艺者之间的合作。沙雷特表示，该器件推翻了古希腊人主要是象牙塔思想家的传统观念，认为他们不会屈于尊严，弄脏他们的双手去做技术性的东西。他说，这是一个提醒，虽然对历史的研究侧重于书面文本，但它们只可以告诉我们在一个特定的时间里发生的事情的一小部分。
I      Imagine a future historian encountering philosophy texts written in our time – and an aircraft engine. The books would tell that researcher what a few scholars were thinking today, but the engine would give them a far better window into how technology influenced our everyday lives. Charette said it was unlikely that the device was used by practitioners of astrology, then still in its infancy. More likely, he said, it was bound for a mantelpiece in some rich Roman’s home. Given that astronomers of the time already knew how to calculate the positions of the sun and the moon and to predict eclipses without the device, it would have been the equivalent of a device built for a planetarium today – something to spur popular interest, or at least claim bragging rights.
I 想象一下，未来的历史学家遇到写在我们这个时代的哲学文本—一还有一个飞机发动机。书会告诉未来的研究员今天的少数学者的思想，但发动机会给他们一个更好的窗口，了解技术是如何影响我们日常生活的。沙雷特表示，该设备被占星术从业人员使用的可能性不大，因为它仍处于起步阶段。更有可能的是，他说，这是一些富有的罗马家庭中壁炉台的装饰。鉴于那时的天文学家已经知道如何计算太阳和月亮的位置，并无需器件来预测日食，那么它会一直相当于今天天文馆内的一个设备—一吸引注意力的东西，或至少也被用来炫耀权力。
J      Why was the technology that went into the device lost? “The time this was built, the jackboot of Rome was coming through,” Edmunds said. “The Romans were good at town planning and sanitation but were not known for their interest in science.” The fact that the device was so complex, and that it was being shipped with a quality of other luxury items, tells Edmunds that it is very unlikely to have been the only one ever made. 「Q24」 “Its sophistication is such that it can’t have been the only one,” 「Q24」 Edmunds said. “There must have been a tradition of making them. We’re always hopeful a better one will surface.” Indeed, he said, he hopes that his study and the renewed interest in the Antikythera Mechanism will prompt second looks by both amateurs and professionals around the world. “The archaeological world may look in their cupboards and maybe say, “That isn’t a bit of rusty old metal in the cupboard.”
J 为什么设备中的技术丢失了?“开始建造这个设备时罗马正处于军事发展阶段，”Edmunds说。“罗马人在城市规划和卫生方面做得很好，但他们对科学的兴趣却很少有人知晓。”事实上，该设备是如此复杂，它与高质量的奢侈品一起被运输，这告诉Edmunds，这不太可能是有史以来唯一一个。“它如此复杂，不可能是唯一的一个，”Edmunds说，“一定有制造它们的传统。 我们总是希望能发现更好的设备。”事实上，他说，他希望他对安提凯希拉机器的研究和新的兴趣会促使世界各地的业余爱好者和专业人士对这一设备多关注一点。 “考古界人士可能会在他们的橱柜里看，也许会说，橱柜里没有一点生锈的旧金属。”
