欧洲滚滚热浪
A IT was the summer, scientists now realise, when felt. 「Q28」 We knew that summer 2003 was remarkable: global warming at last made itself wellknown. Britain experienced its record high temperature and continental Europe saw forest fires raging out of control, great rivers drying of a trickle and thousands of heat-related deaths. But just how remarkable is only now becoming clean.

A 夏天的到来让科学家们有所察觉。众所周知，2003年的夏季与以往不同：全球变暖终于使得英国经历了创纪录的高温，欧洲大陆经历了失控的森林大火，大量河流干涸，热浪使得成千上万的人丧失了生命。但只有现在，情况的严重性才得到重视。
B The three months of June, July and August were the warmest ever recorded in western and central Europe, with record national highs in Portugal, Germany and Switzerland as well as Britain. And they were the warmest by a very long way. 「Q14」 Over a great rectangular block of the earth stretching from west of Paris to northern Italy, taking in Switzerland and southern Germany, the average temperature for the summer months was 3.78℃ above the long-term norm, said the Climatic Research Unit (CRU) of the University of East Anglia in Norwich, which is one of the world’s leading institutions for the monitoring and analysis of temperature records.

B 在欧洲西部和中部，6月，7月和8月这三个月是有史以来最热的一年，葡萄牙、德国和瑞士同样出现与英国一样的高温。在矩形块的地球版上，从巴黎西部延伸到意大利北部，这几个国家形成最热的一条长沿线。从长期的范围来看，瑞士和德国南部夏季的平均气温是3.78℃以上，诺里奇东安格利亚大学的气候研究组（CRU）表示，该组织是全球温度记录监测和分析的领先机构之一。
C That excess might not seem a lot until you are aware of the context - but then you realise it is enormous. There is nothing like this in previous data, anywhere. It is considered so exceptional that Professor Phil Jones, the CRU’s director, is prepared to say openly - in a way few scientists have done before - 「Q16」 that the 2003 extreme may be directly attributed, not to natural climate variability, but to global warming caused by human actions.

C 这看起来似乎超过的并不是很多，但当你意识到研究范围时——你才能体会出它是巨大的。在以前的任何地方都没有像这样的数据。这种情况如此特殊以至于CRU的主任菲尔琼斯教授准备公开表示—以前很少有科学家这么做—导致2003年极端天气的直接原因并不是自然气候的变化，而是由人类行为引起的全球变暖。
D Meteorologists have hitherto contented themselves with the formula that recent high temperatures are consistent with predictions of climate change. For the great block of the map - that stretching between 35-50N and 0-20E - the CRU has reliable temperature records dating back to 1781. Using as a baseline the average summer temperature recorded between 1961 and 1990, departures from the temperature norm, or “anomalies”: over the area as a whole can easily be plotted. As the graph shows, such is the variability of our climate that over the past 200 years, there have been at least half a dozen anomalies, in terms of excess temperature - the peaks on the graph denoting very hot years - approaching, or even exceeding, 20℃. But there has been nothing remotely like 2003, when the anomaly is nearly four degrees.

D 迄今为止，气象学家满足于公式定理，认为最近的高温与气候变化的预测一致。对于巨大的地球板块—伸展在35-50N和0-20E之间—CRU有可靠的温度记录，这些记录可以追溯到1781年。以1961年至1990年间记录的平均夏季温度为基准，抛开温度标准，或“异常情况”，以该地区作为一个整体可以很容易地绘制图表。上面的图表显示，这就是我们气候的变化，在过去的200年里，至少有六种异常情况出现，就超高温度而言，图上表示非常炎热的年份的峰值接近甚至超过20℃。但没有任何一年像2003年，异常时近四度。
E 「Q15」 “This is quite remarkable,” Professor Jones told The Independent. “It’s very unusual in a statistical sense. If this series had a normal statistical distribution, you wouldn’t get this number. The period “how often it could be expected to recur” would be something like one in a thousand years. If we look at an excess above the average of nearly four degrees, then perhaps nearly three degrees of that is natural variability, because we’ve seen that in past summers. But the final degree of it is likely to be due to global warming, caused by human actions.

F The summer of 2003 has, in a sense, been one that climate scientists have long been expecting. 「Q18」 Until now, the warming has been manifesting itself mainly in winters that have been less cold than in summers that have been much hotter. 「Q19」 Last week, the United Nations predicted that winters were warming so quickly that winter sports would die out in Europe’s lower-level ski resorts. 「Q16」 But sooner or later the unprecedented hot summer was bound to come, and this year it did.

F 2003年的夏天，在某种意义上，是气候科学家们长期以来一直期待的。直到现在，变暖的主要特点是冬天变暖和，夏季变得更加炎热。上周，联合国预测，冬季变暖速度加快，冬季运动将在欧洲较低级别的滑雪胜地消失。前所未有的炎热夏天迟早会来临，而今年就来了。
G One of the most dramatic features of the summer was the hot nights, especially in the first half of August. In Paris, the temperature never dropped below 23℃ (73.40°F) at all between 7 and 14 August, and the city recorded its warmest-ever night on 11-12 August, when the mercury did not drop below 25.50℃ (77.90°F). Germany recorded its warmest-ever night at Weinbiet in the Rhine valley with a lowest figure of 27.60℃(80.60°F) on 13 August, and similar record-breaking night-time temperatures were recorded in Switzerland and Italy.

G 夏天最引人注目的特征之一是炎热的夜晚，尤其是在8月的上中旬。在巴黎，8月7-14日期间，温度不低于23℃（73.40°F），并且该城市在8月11-12日创下有史以来最热晚上的记录，温度不低于25.50℃（77.90°F）。德国8月13日在莱茵河谷的魏因比特创下有史以来最炎热夜晚记录，最低温度为27.60℃（80.60°F），瑞士和意大利的夜间气温也创下了相似的纪录。
H 「Q22」 The 15, 000 excess deaths in France during August, compared with previous years, have been related to the high night-time temperatures. The number gradually increased during the first 12 days of the month, peaking at about 2, 000 per day on the night of 12-13 August, then fell off dramatically after 14 August when the minimum temperatures fell by about 50°F. The elderly were most affected, with a 70 per cent increase in mortality rate in those aged 75-94.

H 与前几年相比，8月期间法国新增了15000的死亡人数，这与夜间高温有关。这个月前12天死亡数量逐渐增加，在8月12日至13日晚峰值甚至达到每天死亡2000人，然后在8月14日最低气温下降约50°F后急剧下降。老年人受到的影响最大，75-94岁的老人死亡率增加了70％
I For Britain, the year as a whole is likely to be the warmest ever recorded, but despite the high temperature record on 10 August, 「Q20」 the summer itself - defined as the June, July and August period - still comes behind 1976 and 1995, when there were longer periods of intense heat. 「Q23」 「Q25」 「Q24」 At the moment, the year is on course to be the third-hottest ever in the global temperature record, which is equal to the record in 1856, behind 1998 and 2002 but when all the records for October, November and December are collated, it might move into second place, Professor Jones said. 「Q26」 The 10 hottest years in the record have all now occurred since 1990. Professor Jones is in no doubt about the astonishing nature of European summer of 2003. “The temperatures recorded were out of all proportion to the previous record,” he said. 「Q27」 “It was the warmest summer in the past 500 years and probably way beyond that it was enormously exceptional.”

I 对于英国而言，这一年可能是有史以来最热的一年，尽管在8月10日有高温纪录，但夏季时间长度范围为6月，7月和8月期间，因此较长时间的高温记录仍然是在1976和1995。到目前为止，今年将是有史以来全球第三次记录高温，高温记录可以追溯到1856年，今年排在1998年和2002年之后，但当10月，11月和12月的所有记录被整理后，它可能进入第二位，琼斯教授说。纪录中最热的10年都是在1990年以后。琼斯教授对2003年欧洲夏季的炎热程度毫不意外。 “现在记录的温度与以前的记录不成比例，”他说。 “这是500年来最炎热的夏天，说它非常特殊一点都不夸张。”
J His colleagues at the University of East Anglia’s Tyndall Centre for Climate Change Research are now planning a special study of it. “It was a summer that has not been experienced before, either in terms of the temperature extremes that were reached, or the range and diversity of the impacts of the extreme heat,” said the centred executive director, Professor Mike Hulme. “It will certainly have left its mark on a number of countries, as to how they think and plan for climate change in the future, 「Q21」 much as the 2000 floods have revolutionised the way the Government is thinking about flooding in the UK. “The 2003 heat wave will have similar repercussions across Europe.”

J 他在东安格利亚大学廷德尔气候变化研究中心的同事现在正在计划对此进行一项特别研究。该中心执行主任Mike Hulme教授表示：“我们从未经历过这样的夏天，无论是从达到的极端温度方面来看，还是从极端高温的影响范围和多样性方面来看，” “这肯定会在一些国家中留下印记，至于他们如何思考和规划未来的气候变化，就像2000年的洪水已经彻底改变了英国政府对水灾的思维方式一样。 2003年的热浪将在整个欧洲产生类似的影响。”
