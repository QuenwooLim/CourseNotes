The Study of Teens

青少年研究

A  「Q18」  Our ancestor, Homo Erectus, may not have had culture or even language, but did they have teenagers? For reasons unknown, humans continue to be the only animals on the Earth to have a teenage phase. Determining exactly when adolescence first evolved and the sorts of changes in our bodies during adolescence as well as the lifestyles associated with it, could help us understand the reasons for adolescence. It would be interesting to learn why we alone have a growth spurt so late in life.

我们的祖先直立人可能没有文化，甚至没有语言，但他们有青少年阶段吗?由于未知的原因，人类仍然是地球上唯一有青春期的动物。准确地确定青春期最初形成的时间，确定我们在青春期身体的变化以及与之相关的生活方式，可以帮助我们理解青春期存在的原因。了解为什么只有我们人类在生命中这么晚才出现快速成长是很有趣的。

B 「Q14」    Until recently, the dominant explanation was that physical growth is delayed by our need to grow large brains and to learn all the behaviour patterns associated with humanity一speaking, social interaction, and so on. While such behaviour is still developing, humans can not easily fend for themselves at a young age, and a youthful appearance will make it more likely for parents and other adults to continue looking after younger ones who are still in the major stages of development. 「Q19」  What's more, studies of mammals show a strong relationship between brain size and the rate of development, with larger-brained animals taking longer to reach adulthood. Humans are at the far end of this spectrum. If this theory is correct and the development of large brains accounts for the teenage growth spurt, the origin of adolescence should have been evident in the evolution of Homo sapiens and Neanderthals almost 200, 000 years ago. But, surprisingly, the fossil evidence does not support the existence of adolescence.

直到最近，最主要的解释是，因为我们需要长出更大的大脑，并学习所有与人类语言、社会交往等相关的行为模式，所以身体发育被推迟了。当这些行为仍在发展中人类在年轻时不能轻易地照顾自己，年轻的外表会使父母和其他成年人更有可能继续照顾仍处于发展主要阶段的年轻的孩子。另外，对哺乳动物的研究表明，大脑大小和发育速度之间有很强的关系，脑容量大的动物需要更长的时间才能成年。人类的成年时间在这个范围的较远的一个时间点。如果这个理论是正确的，并且大的大脑的发展导致了青少年的快速成长，那么青春期的起源应该在大约20万年前智人和尼安德特人的进化中就已经很明显了。但令人惊讶的是，化石证据并不支持当时青春期的存在。

C  「Q15」   The human fossil record is extremely sparse, and the number of fossilised children is minuscule. That being said, anthropologists have recently begun to try to find out if we can learn anything about our ancestors from the remains of these children. 「Q20」 Among the most studied is the famous Turkana boy, an almost complete skeleton of Homo Erectus from 1.6 million years ago found in Kenya in 1984. However, it is not easy to determine someone's age from a skeleton. Even with a modem skeleton, you can only make a guesstimate of the age based on the developmental stage of teeth, bones, and skeleton size.

人类的化石记录极其稀少，儿童化石的数量微乎其微。话虽如此，人类学家最近已开始试图找出我们是否能从这些孩子的遗骸中了解到有关我们祖先的任何信息。研究最多的是著名的图尔卡纳男孩，这是1984年在肯尼亚发现的一具几乎完整的直立人骨架，距今已有160万年。然而从骨架上判断一个人的年龄是不容易的。即使是现代的骨骼，你也只能根据牙齿、骨骼和骨架大小的发育阶段来估测它的年龄。

D   「Q21」 You need as many developmental markers as possible to get an estimate of age. The Turkana's teeth made him 10 or 11 years old. The features of his skeleton put him at 13；however, he was as tall as a modern 15-year-old. 「Q24」 Susan Anton, a New York University anthropology professor refers to a research carried out by Margaret Clegg who studied a collection of 18th-century and 19th-century skeletons whose ages at death were known. When Anton tried to age the skeletons without checking the records, she found similar discrepancies to those of the Turkana boy. For example, one 10-year-old boy had the dental age of a 9-year-old； his skeleton was thought to be of a 6-year-old yet had the height of an 11-year-old. “The Turkana kid still has a rounded skull and needs more growth to reach the adult shape, ”Anton adds. She thinks that the Homo Erectus had already developed modern human growth patterns, with a late adolescent growth spurt that was not as drastic as the growth spurts of modem day adolescents She believes that the Turkana boy was just about to enter adolescence.

要估计年龄需要尽可能多的发育指标。图尔卡纳的牙齿可判断他为10或11岁。从他的骨骼特征来看，他有13岁；然而，他和现在15岁的孩子一样高。纽约大学人类学教授苏珊·安东提到了玛格丽特·克莱格进行的一项关于已知死亡年龄的18世纪和19世纪骨架的研究。安东试图在不检查记录的情况下对这些骨骼进行年龄鉴定时发现了与图尔卡纳男孩类似的差异。例如，一个10岁的男孩有9岁孩子的牙齿；有6岁孩子的骨架，但却有11岁孩子的身高。“图尔卡纳小孩的头骨还是圆圆的，需要更多的发育才能长成成人的样子”，安东补充道。她认为直立人已经形成了现代人类的生长模式，已经有了不像现代青少年那样剧烈的青春期后期的快速成长。她相信图尔卡纳男孩即将进入青春期。

E   If Anton is right, her theory differs from the conventional thinking that linked late growth with the development of a large brain. Anthropologist Steven Leigh from the University of Illinois goes further. He believes the idea of adolescence as a catch-up growth does not explain why the growth rate increases so dramatically 「Q25」 . He says that many apes have growth spurts in particular body regions that are associated with reaching maturity, and this makes sense, because by timing the short but crucial spells of maturation to coincide with the seasons when food is plentiful, they minimise the risk of being without adequate food supplies while growing. What makes humans unique is that the whole skeleton is involved.  For Leigh, this is the key.

如果安东是对的，那么她的理论就不同于传统的将发育迟缓与大脑发育联系起来的理论。伊利诺斯大学的人类学家史蒂芬·利则更进了一步。他认为，将青春期视为一种追赶性成长的想法，并不能解释成长速度为何会大幅上升。他说许多猿在与达到成熟相关的身体特定区域出现快速发育，这是有道理的，因为通过把短暂而关键的成熟期与食物充足的季节相配合，生长期没有足够的食物供应这种风险可以降到最低。人类的独特之处在于整个骨骼都参与其中。对Leigh来说，这是关键。

F   According to his theory, adolescence evolved as an integral part of efficient upright locomotion, and to accommodate more complex brains. We have learned from fossils that our ancestors walked on two legs about six million years ago. If proficient walking was important for survival, perhaps the teenage growth spurt has very ancient origins. While many anthropologists will consider Leigh's theory stretches too far, he is not the only one with new ideas about the evolution of teenagers.

根据他的理论，青春期是高效直立运动的一个组成部分，并且适应更复杂的大脑。我们从化石得知，我们的祖先大约在六百万年前用两条腿走路。如果说熟练的走路对生存是重要的，那么青少年的快速成长也许有着非常古老的起源。虽然许多人类学家会认为利的理论太过牵强，但他并不是唯一一个对青少年进化有新想法的人。

G   Surprisingly, the study of tooth growth, has helped anthropologists in their studies regarding teenage evolution 「Q22」 . Every nine days or so, the growing teeth of both apes and humans acquire ridges on their enamel surface. Similar to rings in a tree trunk, the number of ridges in teeth can tell us how long it took the crown of a tooth to form. With mammals, the rate at which teeth develop is correlated with how fast the brain grows and the age at which the mammal matures 「Q16」 . Teeth are good indicators of life history because their growth is less related to the environment and nutrition than the growth of the skeleton.

令人惊讶的是，对牙齿生长的研究促进了人类学家对青少年进化的研究。每隔九天左右，类人猿和人类的牙齿都会在釉质表面形成隆起。就像树干上的年轮一样，牙齿上的齿脊数量可以告诉我们牙齿的顶部形成需要多长时间。对于哺乳动物来说，牙齿发育的速度和大脑发育的速度与哺乳动物成熟的年龄有关。牙齿是生命史的良好指标，因为比起骨骼的生长，它们(牙齿)的生长受环境和营养的影响少。

H   The published findings based on a study of Neanderthal teeth by researchers in France and Spain offered a more decisive piece of evidence 「Q26」 . The Neanderthals had a faster tooth growth than the Homo Erectus who were before them, and hence, possibly, a shorter childhood as well. 「Q23」 Lead researcher Fernando Ramirez-Rozzi thinks the average life expectancy for Neanderthals was 25 years. This was mainly caused by the hostile environment of the glacial Europe where it was usually cold and harsh. 「Q17」 Both the Neanderthals and Homo Erectus evolved to grow up faster than their immediate ancestors. They probably had to reach adulthood fairly quickly without delaying for an adolescent growth spurt.

法国和西班牙的研究人员对尼安德特人的牙齿进行了研究，他们发表的研究结果提供了一个更具决定性的证据。尼安德特人的牙齿生长速度比在他们之前的直立人要快，因此，他们的童年也可能更短。首席研究员费尔南多认为尼安德特人的平均寿命是25岁。这主要是由于欧洲冰川的恶劣环境，那里通常是寒冷和严酷的。尼安德特人和直立人都比他们的直系祖先成长得更快。他们可能必须很快地长大成人，不因为青春期的发育而延迟。
