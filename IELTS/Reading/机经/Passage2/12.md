Biology of Bitterness

苦味生物学
A Have you ever wondered why grapefruit juice is often served in small glasses? It is because most people like to drink it only in small doses, as they find it somewhat bitter. 「Q21-22」 Naringin, a natural chemical found in grapefruit, has a bitter taste. Although some people like that bitterness, there are other people who want nothing to do with it 「Q25」 . So juice companies often decide to use grapefruit with lower naringin content, despite the fact that naringin has antioxidant properties that can help prevent cancer and arteriosclerosis.

你有没有想过为什么葡萄柚汁总是装在小玻璃杯里?这是因为大多数人只喜欢少量饮用，觉得有点苦。 「Q21-22」 柚皮苷是一种在葡萄柚中发现的天然化学物质，有苦味。虽然有些人喜欢这种苦味，但也有人不想与之有任何关系。 「Q25」 因此，果汁公司经常决定使用柚皮苷含量较低的葡萄柚，尽管柚皮苷具备有助于预防癌症和动脉硬化的抗氧化特性。
B A New Jersey biotech company, the Linguagen Corporation, has found a way to eliminate the bitter taste in grapefruit juice. In a taste test, the company had participants drink grapefruit juice from two different sets of white paper cups, labeled 304 and 305. Each participant drank from one cup and then the other, cleansing their palates between tastings with water and a soda cracker.  Even the smallest sip from the 304 cups was determined to have the bitter taste commonly associated with grapefruit. The 305 cups were found to be different, however. They still had the sour taste of citrus, but the bitter taste of naringin was gone. The 305 batch had been treated with adenosine monophosphate, also called AMP 「Q14」 , a compound that blocks the bitter taste in foods without affecting the nutrition value of those foods.

Linguagen公司是新泽西的一家生物科技公司，发现了一种消除葡萄柚汁苦味的方法。在一项味觉测试中，公司让参与者分别饮用两套标着304和305的白色纸杯里的葡萄柚汁。每个参与者先喝一杯，然后再喝另一杯，中间通过喝水和吃苏打饼干清洗他们的味觉。即使从标记304的杯中喝最小的一口也能察觉到它有葡萄柚通常有的苦味。然而，标记305的杯子被发现是不同的。这些杯子里仍然有柑橘的酸味，但柚皮苷的苦味已经消失了。标有305的这批产品使用了腺苷一磷酸(也叫AMP) 进行了处理 「Q14」 , 这种化合物可以在不影响食物营养价值的情况下阻止食物的苦味。

C Taste research has become commonplace these days. Food biologists are researching all five basic tastes：sweet, bitter, sour, salty and umami a Japanese term referring to savoriness. 「Q26」 「Q15」 Food biologists are especially interested in bitterness, because there are thousands of bitter-tasting natural compounds that are not currently used as foods. Biologists view these bitter foods as an untapped potential food source for our future needs. Some bitter foods, such as grapefruit and cruciferous vegetables such as cabbage, kale, broccoli, and cauliflower are nutritious despite，and sometimes because of, their bitter-tasting components. Over time, many people have acquired a taste for bitter foods.

如今，口味研究已经变得很普遍。食品生物学家正在研究所有五种基本口味：甜、苦、酸、咸和鲜味(形容味美的日语词)。 「Q26」 「Q15」 食品生物学家对苦味特别感兴趣，因为有成千上万的苦味天然化合物目前还没有被用作食物。生物学家认为这些苦味食物是可以满足我们未来需要的潜在食物来源。一些苦的食物，如葡萄柚和十字花科蔬菜，如卷心菜、羽衣甘蓝、西蓝花和花菜很有营养，有时候是因为它们的苦味成分。随着时间的推移，许多人已经对苦味食物有兴趣。

D 「Q16」 It is important to note that people taste bitterness at different levels. These variances appear to be mostly genetic. 「Q21-22」 About three-fourths of people are sensitive to the taste of the bitter compounds phenylthiocarbamide and 6-n-propylthiouracil. Those people who are sensitive to phenylthiocarbamide seem to be less likely than others to eat cruciferous vegetables, according to Stephen Wooding, a geneticist at the University of Utah. Some people, known as supertasters, are especially sensitive to 6-n-propylthiouracil because they have an unusually high number of taste buds. 「Q23-24」 It is not unusual for supertasters to avoid all kinds of bitter-tasting foods and drinks, including some vegetables, coffee, and dark chocolate. 「Q23-24」 Supertasters, possibly because of their taste proclivities, tend to be thin. They also tend to be less fond of alcoholic drinks, which are often slightly bitter. A research study found that super tasters consume alcoholic beverages, on average, only two to three times a week, compared with five or six times for the average tasters.

「Q16」 值得注意的是，人们品尝苦味的程度是不同的。这些差异似乎主要是遗传的。 「Q21-22」 约四分之三的人对苯硫脲和6-丙基硫脲这两种苦味化合物的味道很敏感。犹他大学的遗传学家斯蒂芬·伍丁表示，对苯硫脲敏感的人似乎比其他人更少吃十字花科蔬菜。有些人被称为味觉超常者，对6-丙基硫氧嘧啶特别敏感，因为他们的味蕾异常多。对于味觉超常者来说，避免所有口味较苦的食物和饮料是很正常的，包括一些蔬菜、咖啡和黑巧克力。味觉超常的人往往比较瘦，可能是由于他们的味觉倾向。他们也不太喜欢酒精饮料，因为这些饮料通常有点苦。一项研究发现，味觉超常者平均每周只喝2到3次酒精饮料，而味觉正常者平均每周喝5到6次。

E 「Q17」 In describing a taste bud, each taste bud contains a small group of receptors that capture the taste molecules. These taste molecules are known as tastants. When a person tastes something bitter, the receptors relay a signal through proteins called G proteins. These G proteins are involved in the perception of bitterness, sweetness, and umami, and were identified and labeled in the 1990s by Linguagen founder Robert Margolskee at the Mount Sinai School of Medicine in New York. This G protein is known as gustducin and it triggers a cascade of chemical reactions that lead to ion changes within the cell. As this happens, the brain gets a signal that the person is eating something bitter.

「Q17」 说起味蕾，每个味蕾都包含一小群受体，负责捕捉味觉分子。这些味觉分子被称为促味物质。当一个人品尝苦味时，受体通过一种叫G蛋白的蛋白质传递信号。这些G蛋白与感知苦味、甜味和鲜味有关，20世纪90年代, Linguagen的创始人罗伯特·马格斯基在纽约西奈山医学院识别并标记它。这种G蛋白被称为味蛋白，它会引发一系列化学反应，导致细胞内离子的变化。当这种情况发生时，大脑会得到一个信号，表明这个人正在吃一些苦的东西。

F In 2000, a group of researchers identified some 30 different kinds of genes that code for bitter-taste receptors. No matter which tastant enters the mouth or which receptor it attaches to, bitterness always tastes the same. 「Q18」 The only things that vary are the intensity of the bitterness and the ways our sense of taste identifies the flavor.
2000年，一组研究人员发现了大约30种不同的为苦味受体编码的基因。无论哪一种促味物质进入口腔或附着在哪个受体上，苦味总是相同的。 「Q18」 唯一不同的是苦味的强度和我们的味觉识别味道的方式。
G After determining how the taste mechanism works, scientists started to research how to interfere with it. As Margolskee noted, AMP contains no bitterness of its own, however，when it is added to foods, it attaches to bitter taste receptors. 「Q19」 As effective as it is, 「Q27」 it has been determined that AMP cannot block every type of bitter taste, probably because it does not attach itself to all 30 of the bitter-taste receptors. As a result, researchers continue to look for other bitter blockers, using a technology called high-throughput screening.

在确定了味觉机制如何工作后，科学家们开始研究如何干扰它。正如马格斯基所指出的, AMP本身不含苦味, 但当它被添加到食物中时，它会附着在苦味感受体上。 「Q19」 尽管AMP有效, 但它不能阻止所有类型的苦味，可能是因为它不能附着在所有30种苦味受体上。因此，研究人员使用一种叫作高通量筛选的技术继续寻找其他的苦味阻滞剂。

H 「Q20」 Taste researchers are excited about the idea that compounds like AMP will eventually make processed foods healthier. For example, a single cup of Campbell's chicken noodle soup contains 850 milligrams of sodium chloride (table salt) , more than a third of the recommended daily allowance. All the salt in the soup masks the bitterness created in the canning process. Part of the salt that is currently used in the soup could be replaced by a different kind of salt，potassium chloride, which tends to be scarce in some diets. Potassium chloride has a bitter aftertaste that could be easily eliminated with a dose of AMP. Bitter blockers could a lobe used to take the bitterness out of things like children's cough syrup and they can also be used to reduce the bitterness which is frequent in other drugs and medications.

「Q20」 味觉研究人员对AMP等化合物能最终使加工食品更健康的想法感到兴奋。例如，一杯金宝汤的鸡肉面汤含有850毫克的氯化钠(食盐)，超过每日推荐摄入量的三分之一汤里的盐掩盖了罐装过程中产生的苦味。目前在汤中使用的部分盐可以被另一种盐取代，即氯化钾，它在一些饮食中往往稀少。氯化钾有一种很容易被AMP去除的苦味。苦味阻滞剂也可以用来去除儿童咳嗽糖浆中的苦味，还可以用来减少其他药物中经常有的苦味。
