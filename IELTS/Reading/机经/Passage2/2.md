A  John Franklin (1786-1847) was the most famous vanisher of the Victorian era. 「Q14」 He joined the Navy as a midshipman at the age of 14, and fought in the battles of Copenhagen and Trafalgar. When peace with the French broke out, he turned his attention to Arctic exploration, and in particular to solving the conundrum of the Northwest Passage, the mythical clear-water route which would, if it existed, link the Atlantic and the Pacific Oceans above the northern coast of the American continent. The first expedition Franklin led to the Arctic was an arduous overland journey from Hudson Bay to the shores of the so-called Polar Ocean east of the Coppermine River. Between 1819 and 1822, Franklin and his twenty-strong team covered 5550 miles on foot. Their expedition was a triumph of surveying–they managed to chart hundreds of miles of previously unknown coastline.


约翰·富兰克林（1786-1847）是维多利亚时代最著名的“消失者”。他14岁时以海军候补生身份加入海军，参加了哥本哈根战役和特拉法加战役。当与法国的和平来临时，他将注意力转向了北极探险，特别是解决西北航道的难题——这是一条传说中的无冰水道，如果存在，它将连接北美大陆北部海岸上方的‌大西洋与太平洋‌。富兰克林领导的第一次北极探险是一次艰苦的陆路旅程，从哈德逊湾到科珀曼河以东的所谓的极地海洋海岸。在1819年至1822年间，富兰克林和他的20人团队徒步旅行了5550英里。他们的探险是测绘工作的胜利——他们成功测绘了数百英里此前未知的海岸线。

B   「Q18」 There followed a career as a travel writer and salon-goer (‘the man who ate his boots’ was Franklin’s tag-line), a second long Arctic expedition, and a controversial spell as Governor of Van Diemen’s Land. Then, in May 1845, Franklin set off with two ships–the Erebus and the Terror – and 129 men on the voyage that would kill him. In July, the convoy was seen by two whalers, entering Lancaster Sound. Nothing more would be heard of it for 14 years. Had the ships sunk or been iced in? Were the men dead, or in need of rescue? Or had they broken through to the legendary open polar sea, beyond the ‘ice barrier’?


之后，他成为旅行作家和沙龙常客（富兰克林的标签是“那个吃自己靴子的人”），进行了第二次漫长的北极探险，并担任了范迪门斯地总督这一有争议的职位。然后，在1845年5月，富兰克林带着两艘船——幽冥号和恐怖号——以及129名船员出发，这次航行将导致他的死亡。7月，两艘捕鲸船看到这支船队进入兰开斯特海峡。此后14年再无音讯。船只是沉没了还是被冰封住了？船员们是死了还是需要救援？或者他们已经突破了“冰障”，进入了传说中的极地开放海域？

C  In this personal correspondence and in his published memoirs, Franklin comes across as a man dedicated to the external duties of war and exploration, who kept introspection and self-analysis to a minimum. His blandness makes him an amenably malleable subject for a novelist, and Sten Nadolny has taken full advantage of this license. 「Q19」 「Q17」 Most important, he has endowed his John Franklin with a defining character trait for which there is no historical evidence: (‘slowness’ or ‘calmness’).

在他的私人通信和出版的回忆录中，富兰克林给人的印象是一个致力于战争和探险的外在事务的人，他将内省和自我分析降到最低。他的平淡使他成为小说家手中易于塑造的题材，斯滕·纳多尔尼充分利用了这种自由度。最重要的是，他赋予他的约翰·富兰克林一个历史上没有证据支持的决定性性格特征：（“缓慢”或“冷静”）。

D  Slowness influences not only Franklin’s behavior, but also his vision, his thought and his speech. 「Q15」 The opening scene of The Discovery of Slowness (The Discovery of Slowness by Sten Nadolny) – depicts Franklin as a young boy, playing catch badly because his reaction time is too slow. 「Q20」 Despite the bullying of his peers, Franklin resolves not to fall into step with ‘their way of doing things’. For Nadolny, Franklin’s fatal fascination with the Arctic stems from his desire to find an environment suited to his peculiar slowness.


缓慢不仅影响富兰克林的行为，还影响他的视野、思想和言语。《发现缓慢》的开篇场景将富兰克林描绘成一个小男孩，因为反应太慢而接球很差。尽管受到同伴的欺负，富兰克林还是决心不陷入“他们的做事方式”。对纳多尔尼来说，富兰克林对北极的致命迷恋源于他渴望找到一个适合他独特缓慢的环境。

E   「Q21」 He describes Franklin as a boy dreaming of the ‘open water and the time without hours and days’ which exist in the far north, and of finding in the Arctic a place ‘where nobody would find him too slow’. 「Q23」 Ice is a slow mover. Ice demands a corresponding patience from those who venture onto it. The explorers who have thrived at high latitudes and at high altitudes haven’t usually been men of great speed. They have tended instead to demonstrate unusual self-possession, a considerable capacity for boredom, and a talent for what the Scots call ‘tholing’, the uncomplaining endurance of suffering. 


他将富兰克林描述为一个梦想着“开阔的水面和没有小时和天的时间”存在于远北地区的男孩，并梦想在北极找到一个“没有人会觉得他太慢”的地方。冰是缓慢的移动者。冰要求那些冒险踏上它的人具备相应的耐心。在高纬度和高海拔地区茁壮成长的探险家通常不是速度很快的人。他们反而倾向于展现出不同寻常的镇定、相当大的忍受无聊的能力，以及苏格兰人所说的“tholing”——无怨无悔地忍受苦难的天赋。

F  These were all qualities which the historical Franklin possessed in abundance, and so Nadolny’s concentration and exaggeration of them isn’t unreasonable. 「Q24」 Even as an adult, his slowness of thought means that he is unable to speak fluently, so he memorizes ‘entire fleets of words and batteries of responses’ and speaks a languid, bric-a-brac language. 「Q25」 In the Navy, his method of thinking first and acting later initially provokes mockery from his fellow sailors. But Franklin persists in doing things his way. and gradually earns the respect of those around him. To a commodore who tells him to speed up his report of an engagement, he replies: ‘When I tell something, sir, I use my own rhythm.’ A lieutenant says approvingly of him: ‘Because Franklin is so slow, he never loses time.’


这些特质恰恰都是真实历史中的富兰克林所充分具备的，因此纳多尔尼对这些特质进行着重刻画与放大，并非毫无道理。即使成年后，他思维缓慢意味着他无法流利地说话，于是他背诵了整套的词句与成套的应答话术，说着一种缓慢的、杂烩式的语言。在海军中，他先思考后行动的方法最初引起了其他水手的嘲笑。但富兰克林坚持用自己的方式做事，逐渐赢得了周围人的尊重。当一位海军准将告诉他加快战事汇报的语速，他回答说：“长官，当我讲述一件事时，自有我的节奏。”一位中尉赞许地说：“正因为富兰克林性子慢，他反倒从不耽误时间。”

G  Since it was first published in Germany in 1983, The Discovery of Slowness has sold more than a million copies and been translated into 15 languages. 「Q22」 It has been named as one of German literature’s twenty ‘contemporary classics’, and it has been adopted as a manual and manifesto by European pressure groups and institutions representing causes as diverse as sustainable development, the Protestant Church, management science, motoring policy and pacifism. 

自1983年在德国首次出版以来，《发现缓慢》已售出超过一百万册，并被翻译成15种语言。它被评为德国文学“当代经典”之一，并被代表各种不同事业的欧洲压力团体和机构采纳为手册和宣言，这些事业包括可持续发展、新教教会、管理科学、汽车政策和和平主义。

Nadolny’s Franklin appeals to them because he is immune to ‘the compulsion to be constantly occupied’, and to the idea that ‘someone was better if he could do the same thing fast.’ Several German churches have used him in their symposia and focus groups as an example of peacefulness, piety and self-confidence. A centre scheme (a ‘march of slowness’ or ‘of the slow’), inspired by the novel. 采用这部小说的各个团体有一个共同点：不喜欢后现代的快节奏文化。纳多尔尼笔下的富兰克林吸引他们，是因为他不受“不断忙碌的强迫”以及“如果一个人能更快地做同一件事，他就更好”这一观念的影响。几家德国教会在他们的研讨会和焦点小组中将他作为平和、虔诚和自信的榜样。一个由这部小说启发的中心计划（“缓慢游行”或“缓慢者游行”）。纳多尔尼曾作为卢塞恩组织RIO的客座演讲者出现，该组织旨在调和管理原则与环境可持续性的理念。这部小说甚至参与了关于德国道路限速的辩论。今天在高速公路上行驶，你会看到路边的大型标志上写着“ tranquility”（宁静）或“unhurriedness”（从容），这是一个故意利用小说标题的标语。
