Sociopolitical Themes of British Architecture

英国建筑的社会政治主题

A Without a doubt, architecture contains elements of evolution and development, rather than revolution and war. A clear example of this concept involves looking at the history of British architecture and debunking myths surrounding the topic. Despite the popular notion that the Roman escape from Britain in the fifth century marked the simple disappearance of engineering phenomena, such as Hadrian’s Wall and expertly lad-out towns, the truth is more complicated. Another myth involves the Norman Conquest of 1066. In that myth, the force is thought to bring back the Gothic cathedrals of the Middle Ages, thus sparking a rebirth in British Culture. Additionally, a large number of the Anglo-Saxon buildings were constructed from wood, resulting in hardly any proof of their elegant style and stunning undertakings.  These ideas, albeit interesting, ignore the fact that Romano-British culture maintained a strong existence even after the Romans pulled out.

毫无疑问，建筑包含着进化和发展的元素，而不是革命和战争。这一概念的一个明显的例子包括回顾英国建筑史和揭开围绕这一主题的谬见。尽管人们普遍认为，罗马人在5世纪逃离英国标志着如哈德良长城和精心布置的城镇等工程现象的消失，但事实比这要复杂得多。另一个谬误涉及1066年的诺曼征服，这个谬误认为是军队带回中世纪哥特式教堂的元素，从而引发了英国文化的重生。此外，盎格鲁一撒克逊人的建筑多数是用木材建造的，因此很难证明他们的优雅风格和惊艳的工作。这些想法虽然有趣，却忽略了一个事实，即罗马―英国文化在罗马人撤出后仍然是一种强大的存在。

B Some of the most wondrous examples of British architecture came about after 1066, when the Normans landed at Pevensey and before 1485, when the Tudors entered the Early Modern period due to the death of Richard III in Bosworth. 「Q27」 The fundamental idea behind medieval architecture can be best described as‘fitness for purpose', meaning that British architecture was not only beautifully elegant but it featured perfectly functional buildings. For example, magnificent castles were ornately styled yet were fit for battle and protection. 「Q35」 However, domestic architecture of the time also featured buildings with a purpose of showcasing one's affluent status in society or financial stability.

英国建筑的一些最奇妙的例子就出现在1066年诺曼人在佩文西登陆之后和1485年都铎王朝因理查德三世在博斯沃思去世而进入现代早期之前。 「Q27」 中世纪建筑的基本理念可以用 “适合用途” 来描述，这意味着建筑不仅美丽优雅，而且功能完善。例如，宏伟的城堡风格华丽，又适合战争和防御。 「Q35」 然而，当时的国内建筑也以显示一个人的社会富裕地位或经济稳定状况的目的为特色。
C The craftsmanship of the 16th Century featured a striking paradox in terms of the arrangement of houses and courtyards. 「Q28」 In previous medieval times, houses faced inward towards one or many courtyards as a way to prevent intruders from entering. 「Q36」 However, it was a different picture in the 16th century when British domestic architecture featured more elaborately decorated, outwad-facing buildings, placing a higher emphasis on security and decorative exteriors. These structures usually included larger rooms, which were often detailed with glass aspects 「Q29」 . The proportional exteriors, grand entrance halls, and decorative features all made quite a flashy statement regarding one's monetary aspects.

16世纪的工艺在房屋和庭院的布置上有一个显著的矛盾。 「Q28」 在以前的中世纪，房屋朝内朝向一个或多个庭院，以保证不受入侵者的侵扰。 「Q36」 然而，在16世纪的英国，情况就不同了，当时的英国家庭建筑的特点是装饰更加精致，建筑朝外，更加强调安全和装饰性的外观。这些建筑通常包含更大的房间，常常用玻璃装饰 「Q29」 。相称的外观、宏伟的入口大厅和装饰功能都在一个人的金钱方面做了一个相当浮华的声明。

D Most architects of the 17th century followed this classic Tudor style, and some continued on to expand upon its dramatic influence. Inigo Jones (1573―1652) , perhaps, is the sole example of how an artist was able to maintain a diverse style when working with classical detail during this period. In any case, the Italian, Dutch, and French architecture styles came  into prominence during the aftermath of the Civil War in the 1640s and 1650s. Circumstances related to the war and Charles II’s exile brought both nobles and gentlemen to the continent and sparked an interest in this“new”architecture. 「Q37」 Thus, when Charles II was restored to the throne in 1660， the influence of European trends was noticeable in the property of royalists，rich with over-the-top, dramatic style. In away, these socio-political details and historical events coined the British Baroque style as an example of absolutist ideology, a way for conflicted citizens in the post-war era to take back their culture.

17世纪的大多数建筑师都沿袭了这种都铎式的经典风格，有些人还继续扩展其戏剧性的影响。依理高·琼斯(1573一1652)可能是这一时期艺术家在处理古典细节时保持多样风格的唯一例子。无论如何，意大利、荷兰和法国的建筑风格在17世纪40年代和50年代的内战余波中崭露头角。与战争相关的情况和查理二世的流放把贵族和绅士都带到了欧洲大陆，并激发了人们对这种“新”建筑的兴趣。 「Q37」 因此，当查理二世在1660年重新登上王位时，欧洲潮流的影响在保皇主义者的房产中得到了明显的体现，它们拥有夸张、戏剧化的风格。在某种程度上，这些社会政治细节和历史事件创造了英国作为绝对主义意识形态的例子的巴洛克风格，是战后抵触的公民收回他们文化的一种方式。

E The acceptance of budding industrial processes can be seen through 19th century architecture, especially with Joseph Poxton's Crystal Palace, which served as the dwelling place of the Great Exhibition of 1851. 「Q30」 Despite the initial acceptance of industrialization, some reformers were unable to accept its dehumanizing consequences. William Morris and John Ruskin are just a few examples of reformers who sought to return“back to basics”. In fact, the 1880s gave way to a new generation of determined architects seeking to follow in Morris's footsteps. 「Q38」 They did so by providing ethical construction methods that were more aligned with pre-industrial manufacturing techniques.
从19世纪的建筑可以看出人们对新兴工业方法的接受，尤其是约瑟夫·波克斯顿设计的 「Q30」 作为1851年世界博览会举办地的水晶宫。尽管人们最初接受了工业化，但这些改革者却无法接受工业化带来的非人性化后果。威廉·莫里斯和约翰·拉斯金是试图“回归基础”的改革者中的几个例子。事实上，19世纪80年代偏 「Q38」 好追随莫里斯脚步的新一代有决心的建筑师。他们通过提供更符合工业化以前制造技术的道德建筑方法来做到这一点。

F These aforementioned “followers” of Morris remained even into the early 20th century when new architects were entering the scene 「Q31」 . 「Q39」 Despite these“establishment architects"obsessed with preserving the feel of the pre-Indutrial Revolution era, the 20th century was home to new architects, such as Gropius, who was familiarising himself with new glass techniques，and Le Corbusier, who dabbled in working with concrete frames 「Q39」 . Britain, however, seemed to be stuck in the past. It is only thanks to foreign architects including Serge Chermayeff，Berthold Lubetkin, and Erno Goldfinger that various Modern Movement buildings were produced during this time. 「Q39」 Thus, only a few British structures made in the 1920s and 1930s are noteworthy.
这些前面提到的莫里斯的“追随者”甚至在20世纪早期仍然存在，当时新建筑师正在进入这个领域。 「Q31」 尽管这些“当权派建筑师”痴迷于保留前工业革命时代的感觉，但20世纪是新建筑师的家园，比如熟悉新的玻璃技术的格罗皮乌斯和涉猎用混凝土框架的勒柯布西耶。然而，英国似乎陷入了过去。在这一时期，各种各样的现代运动建筑都是由塞尔日·切尔马耶夫、伯托尔德·鲁贝金和埃尔诺等外国建筑师设计的。因此，在20世纪20年代和30年代建造的英国建筑中，只有少数值得注意。

G 「Q32」 Just when things seemed stagnant regarding Britain's architectural capabilities. Attlee's Labour government and the Modern Movement prompted the reconstruction phase in 1945 「Q40」 due to an intense demand for affordable housing that could be produced in little to no time. As such, construction focused on practicability over ornate decoration and followed along with the Modernists' use of prefabricated elements, metal frames and concrete. This view was not easily accepted by all.  In 「Q33」 fact, the British were generally skeptical regarding the government’s ability to create effective housing developments and schools.  Nevertheless, the century-long sense of authority in architecture transitioned from private individuals to local authorities.

「Q32」 就在英国的建筑能力似乎停滞不前的时候，艾德礼的工党政府和现代运动推动了1945年的重建阶段， 「Q40」 原因是人们对能在很短时间内建好的廉价房屋的强烈需求。因此，建筑注重实用性而非华丽的装饰，并遵循了现代主义者对预制构件、金属框架和混凝土的使用。这一观点不容易被所有人接受。 「Q33」 事实上，英国人普遍对其政府建立有效的住房和学校的能力持怀疑态度。然而，一个世纪以来，建筑的权威感从个人转向地方部门。

H Interestingly enough, the 1980s presented the Modem Movement as a scapegoat for the so-called social experiments associated with high rise buildings and housing, thus leading the way for postmodernism. Rightly so, many people wonder what could be next for British architecture. By following the post-war trend, it is clear that much remains unchanged regarding the authorities that govern architecture. Since the post-war period, corporations, local authorities, and multinational companies continue to control British architecture.  However, critics and observers alike agree that the Millennium is displaying the unfortunate old age of postmodernism. 「Q34」 Perhaps the appropriate answer for the future of British architecture involve some kind of post-postmodernism, if it exists.

有趣的是，20世纪80年代，现代主义运动成为与高层建筑和住房相关的所谓社会实验的替罪羊，从而为后现代主义开辟了道路。的确，许多人都想知道英国建筑的下一个发展方向。通过遵循战后的趋势，很明显，管理建筑的权威部门仍然没有改变。自战后以来，公司、地方当局和跨国公司继续控制着英国的建筑。然而，评论家和观察家们一致认为，新千年展示了后现代主义的不幸晚年。 「Q34」 对于英国建筑的未来，如果存在某种后后现代主义，或许是一个合适的答案。
