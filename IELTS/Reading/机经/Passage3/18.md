Water Filter

滤水器

An ingenious invention is set to bring clean water to the third world and while the science may be cutting edge, the materials are extremely down to earth.

一项别出心裁的发明将为第三世界带来清洁的水，虽然其科学可能是最前沿的，但它的材料却非常切合实际。

A 「Q34」 Tony Flynn, a materials scientist from Australian National University(ANU) , developed a simple new way of making water filters with commonly available materials, 「Q35」 and these water filters can be fired on the ground using cow manure as a source of heat, without the need for a kiln. The filters have proved the ability to remove common pathogens (disease-producing organisms) such as E-coli. Unlike other water filtering devices, these filters are simple and inexpensive to make. 「Q28」 “They are very simple to explain and demonstrate and can be made by anyone, anywhere 「Q28」 , ”says Flynn “They don't require any advanced technology.  All you need is terracotta clay, a compliant cow, and a match. ”

澳大利亚国立大学的材料科学家托尼·弗林开发了一种简单的使用常见材料制作滤水器的新方法，这些滤水器可以在地面上使用牛粪作为热源进行烧制，不需要窑。 这些过滤器已证明有能力去除常见的病原体(产生疾病的有机体)，如大肠杆菌。与其他水过滤设备不同，这些过滤器制作简单，造价低廉。“它们非常容易解释和演示，任何人、任何地方都可以制作，弗林说。“它们不需要任何先进技术。你所需要的只是陶土，一头顺从的牛和一根火柴。”

B 「Q36」 The production of the filters is extremely simple：take a handful of dry, crushed clay and mix it with a kind of organic material such as used tea leaves, coffee grounds, or rice hulls, and add enough water to make a stiff biscuit-like mixture and form a cylindrical pot with one end closed, and let it dry in the sun. 「Q27」 According to Flynn, used coffee grounds have given the best result to date. Next, surround the post with straw, placing them in a pile of cow manure. Then light the straw and top up the burning manure as required. In less than an hour, the filters are ready. The walls of the finished pot should have the thickness similar to that of an adult index finger. 「Q37」 The properties of cow manure are vital as the fuel can reach a temperature of 700 degrees and above in half an hour, peaking at 950 degrees after another 20 to 30 minutes. The manure makes a good fuel because it is very high in organic material that burns readily and quickly； the manure only works when it is dry and is best used exactly as found in the field； there is no need to change its form or process further.

这种过滤器的生产非常简单：取少量的干的碎土，然后把它和一种有机物质如用过的茶叶、咖啡渣，或稻壳混合物，并加入足够的水来做一个硬的饼干状的混合物，然后使之形成一端封闭的圆柱形罐，在阳光下让它干燥。据弗林说，用过的咖啡渣迄今为止效果最好。接下来，用稻草把它围起来，放在牛粪堆里， 然后点燃稻草，根据需要补充燃烧的牛粪。不到一个小时，过滤器就准备好了。成品容器壁的厚度应与成人食指的厚度相似。牛粪的特性是至关重要的，因为这种燃料在半小时内可以达到700度及以上，再过20到30分钟将会达到950度。粪肥是一种很好的燃料，因为它含有很高的有机物质，燃烧迅速。这种肥料只有在干燥的时候才会发挥作用，而且最好是像在田地里一样使用，没有必要改变它的形状或进一步加工。

C “ 「Q38」 For one thing, a potter's kiln is an expensive item. For another, it could take up to four or five hours to get up to 800 degrees. Besides, 「Q29」 to make it work, fuel that is expensive or scarce such as gas or wood will be needed, plus the experience to run it. 「Q29」 With no technology，no insulation and nothing other than a pile of cow manure, and a match, none of these restrictions apply, ”Flynn says.

“一方面，陶窑是一件昂贵的物品。另一方面，可能需要4到5个小时才能达到800度。此外，为了使它工作，需要昂贵或稀缺的燃料，如天然气或木材，以及让陶窑运作起来的经验。若没有技术，没有绝缘材料，只使用一堆牛粪和火柴，这些（针对陶窑的）限制也都不存在了，”弗林说。

D One of the reasons why manure is a desired material to be used in making these filters is because cow dung is so readily available in most areas of the planet. “A cow is a natural fuel factory. My understanding is that cow dung as a fuel would be pretty much the same wherever you would find it. ”Just as using manure as a fuel for domestic uses is not a new idea, the porosity of clay is something that potters have known about for years, and something that, as a former ceramics lecturer in the ANU School of Art, Flynn is well aware of. 「Q31」 The difference is that rather than viewing the porous nature of the material as a problem, it must be viewed as a solution—after all, not many people want a pot that won't hold water； his filters capitalise on this property.
牛粪是制造这些过滤器的理想材料，原因之一是牛粪在地球上大部分地区都很容易得到。“牛是天然的燃料工厂。我的理解是，牛粪作为燃料在任何地方都是一样的。”正如使用粪肥作为家庭使用的燃料并不是一个新概念，陶工们多年前就知道黏土的多孔性，而弗林作为澳大利亚国立大学艺术学院原陶艺讲师也很清楚这一点。不同之处在于，与其将材料的多孔性视为一个问题，不如将其视为一个解决方案——毕竟，没有多少人想要一个装不了水的罐子。他的过滤器利用了这一特性。

E Now with Flynn's method, the filtration process is simple but effective. 「Q33」 The basic principle is that there are passages through the filter that are wide enough for water droplets to pass through, but too narrow for pathogens. For example, tests with the deadly E-coli bacterium have shown that the filters will remove 96.4% to 99.8% of the pathogens, which is certainly within the safe levels. The use of organic material which burns away leaving cavities after firing helps produce the structure in which pathogens will become trapped. This ove 「Q30」 rcomes the potential problems of finer clay that may not let water through or the problem that cracks are too large to filter pathogens. Much like clay and cow dung, organic material is universally available.

用弗林的方法，过滤过程简单，便宜且有效。其基本原理是，贯穿过滤器的通道大小，足以让液滴通过，但不足以让病原体通过。例如，对致命的大肠杆菌的测试表明，过滤器可以去除96.4%到99.8%的病原体，这当然是在安全水平之内的。有机材料在燃烧后会留下空洞，使用有机材料有助于形成可以使病原体被困住的结构。这就克服了细黏土的潜在问题，即可能不让水通过，或者裂缝过大无法过滤病原体。就像黏土和牛粪一样，有机材料也随处可见。

F This invention was spawned from a World Vision project involving the Manatuto community in East Timor. 「Q39」 The charity wanted to help set up a small industry manufacturing water filters， but initial research found the local clay to be too fine—a problem solved by the addition of organic material. Although the problems of producing a working ceramic filter in East Timor were eventually overcome 「Q32」 ， the Timor solution was based on the use of a kiln and materials that were only native to Timor. Manure firing with no requirement for a kiln has made this zero technology approach available anywhere it is needed. With all the components being widely available， 「Q40」 Flynn， who has no plans to patent his idea, believes that there is no reason why this method couldn't be applied throughout the developing world. “Clean water should not be only accessible to a small group of people. With the help of these filters, people in any country would drink water safely， ”says Flynn.

这项发明由东帝汶马纳图托社区参与的世界宣明会计划所产生。该慈善机构想帮助建立一个小型生产滤水器的行业，但最初的研究发现当地的黏土太细了——添加有机材料就解决了这个问题。虽然在东帝汶生产有效的陶瓷过滤器的问题最终得到解决，但帝汶的解决办法是以使用窑为基础，也以帝汶特有的本土材料为基础。不需要窑炉就可以烧粪肥，这使得这种零技术在任何需要的地方都可以使用。弗林说，由于所有的组件都可以广泛得到，这个方法没有理由不能在所有发展中国家得以应用，而且他也没有为他的想法申请专利的计划。“清洁水不应该只被一小部分人得到。在这些过滤器的帮助下，任何国家的人都可以安全地喝水。”弗林说。
