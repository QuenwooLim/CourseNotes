Robert Louis Stevenson
The writer of some of the best known stories in the English language, including Treasure Island and the Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde.

罗伯特・路易斯・史蒂文森   英国文学史上多部传世经典故事的作者，代表作有《金银岛》《化身博士》。

A  It is more than 100 years since the death of the Scottish writer Robert Louis Stevenson on the South Pacific island of Samoa. And it seems that time has not been kind to Stevenson’s memory. Immediately after his death, his family and friends set to work to fashion the legend of Robert Louis Stevenson, or R.L.S. as he became known – one of the few writers familiar from his initials alone. Subsequent works of biography then turned him into a writer of almost religious importance. One example was history critic Balfour, who in 1901 portrayed Stevenson’s family as ministering angels to the dying genius during his final illness. 「Q27」 Similarly, the biographer Crouch absurdly overstated Stevenson’s significance by placing him in the same company as those most revered names in English literature, Shakespeare and Keats. The reaction to this nonsense was a number of highly critical assessments of Stevenson’s legacy in the 1920s. 

A  苏格兰作家罗伯特・路易斯・史蒂文森在南太平洋萨摩亚群岛离世，至今已逾百年。岁月似乎并未善待世人对他的追忆。他刚去世，亲友便着手为他塑造传奇形象，世人多简称他为 R.L.S.，他也是为数不多仅凭姓名首字母缩写就广为人知的作家之一。此后各类传记作品，更是将他塑造成近乎圣人般的文学巨匠。历史学家兼评论家鲍尔弗便是一例，1901 年他在传记中写道，史蒂文森晚年病重时，其家人如同守护天使一般照料这位天才。无独有偶，传记作家克劳奇也过分夸大了史蒂文森的文学地位，将他与英国文学史上最受尊崇的莎士比亚、济慈并列。面对这类牵强附会的吹捧，20 世纪 20 年代涌现出大量批判史蒂文森文学价值的评论观点。

B  Normally, the critical pendulum can be relied on to swing back again, but there are several aspects of Stevenson’s work that have, until recently, acted against a more balanced appraisal. First is the allegation that Stevenson was a mere master of linguistic fireworks who lacked moral depth. 「Q28」 Some critics accused him of being a literary charlatan of juggling words very prettily to strike effects which overawed an ignorant public, and served to distract from the inadequacy of his ideas. 
B  文学评论的风向向来会来回转变，但长久以来，有几方面因素始终阻碍着人们客观公允地评价史蒂文森的作品。首先，有人指责他只是擅长辞藻华丽的文字技巧，缺乏思想与道德深度。部分评论家斥其为文学投机之徒：仅靠精巧雕琢文字营造华丽效果，震慑普通读者，却刻意掩盖自身思想内涵的匮乏。

C  Then there has been a prejudice against the adventure story as the proper medium for deep moral seriousness, a prejudice which is still extremely influential today. 「Q29」 It seems that we can accept that an adventure film can successfully express profound moral truths, but we reject the same idea for a book. The absurdity of this becomes apparent when we think of writers like Joseph Conrad and Graham Greene, but it is no use pretending that this bias against adventure stories is not part of our high culture. 「Q30」 A further problem is that Stevenson has often not found favour in the land of his birth because his conservatism so often collides with the strong radical tradition in Scotland. His many escapist stories and preference for living abroad have led to accusations that he camouflaged Scotland’s real problems. 「Q31」 Lastly, the high adventure of Stevenson’s own lifestyle has sometimes obscured his output. His globe-trotting, and above all the final phase of his life in Samoa, tended to make his own life a greater story than any he could devise. This was precisely what his friends feared would happen towards the end of his short life: his art might be overwhelmed by the drama of life in Samoa. 

C  其次，文学界一直存有固有偏见，认为冒险小说无法承载深刻的道德内涵，这种偏见至今仍根深蒂固。人们可以接受冒险电影诠释深刻的人生哲理，却不愿认同冒险小说也具备同样价值。只要想到约瑟夫・康拉德、格雷厄姆・格林这类作家，就能看出这种观点的荒谬，但不可否认，轻视冒险小说的偏见早已根植于精英文化之中。再者，史蒂文森在故土苏格兰始终不受认可，他的保守思想与苏格兰浓厚的激进传统格格不入。他创作大量逃避现实的冒险故事，又常年旅居海外，因此被指责刻意回避、粉饰苏格兰的现实社会问题。最后，史蒂文森本人跌宕传奇的人生经历，也时常掩盖了他的文学成就。他一生周游世界，尤其是晚年定居萨摩亚的岁月，他的人生故事甚至比自己笔下的任何作品都更富戏剧性。在他短暂人生的最后时光，友人早已担忧：萨摩亚的传奇生活会掩盖他的文学创作本身。

D  One consequence of this has been that Stevenson’s influence on other writers has too often been neglected. The writer and poet Oscar Wilde was deeply influenced by Stevenson, 「Q32」 even though he declared that Stevenson would have produced better work if he had lived in London rather than Samoa. 「Q34」 Stevenson tends to stick in the throat even of those writers who would like to spit him out, 「Q33」 such as Shaw, who claimed to have learned from him that the romantic hero is always mocked by reality. Likewise, the writer Galsworthy, who being a determined critic, 「Q35」 later changed his mind and said that the superiority of Stevenson over the novelist Hardy was that Stevenson was all life and Hardy, all death. The influence on the novelist Chesterton would also repay detailed study, for it was through him that Stevenson has managed to cross the ages emerging as an influence on the modernist movement and our own contemporary Latin American school of magical realism. 

D  受以上种种因素影响，史蒂文森对后世作家的深远影响长期被忽视。作家、诗人奥斯卡・王尔德深受其熏陶，即便王尔德曾坦言，若史蒂文森定居伦敦而非萨摩亚，定能创作出更优秀的作品。就连萧伯纳这类本想刻意排斥他的作家，也无法摆脱其影响；萧伯纳坦言，自己从史蒂文森作品中领悟到：浪漫主义英雄终究会被现实嘲弄。同样，向来立场坚定的评论家、作家高尔斯华绥后来也改变看法，直言史蒂文森远胜小说家哈代：史蒂文森的文字满是鲜活生命力，而哈代的作品只剩死寂落寞。小说家切斯特顿受其影响之深，也值得深入研究；正是经由切斯特顿，史蒂文森的文学价值跨越时代，既影响了现代主义文学运动，也滋养了当代拉美魔幻现实主义文学流派。

E  When making an assessment of his life and work, one question must inevitably be asked: was Robert Louis Stevenson Scotland’s greatest writer of English prose? For most commentators this honour falls to Sir Walter Scott, author of Ivanhoe among many other classic novels, and it is true that in terms of craftsmanship, precision and the ability to minutely regulate language to create the desired effect, Scott takes the prize. 「Q37」 However, this is not the same thing at all as inherent talent: by way of comparison one may take the example of the two great Russian composers Shostakovich and Prokofiev, of whom the former had learned more precise skills of execution but 「Q37」 the latter’s intrinsic genius was greater, and so it seems to be with Scott and Stevenson. 「Q38」 Admittedly, Scott’s detailed style does permit his stories to explore levels of tragedy that are beyond Stevenson’s reach, but in this regard they have the musty smell of the museum, somehow artificial and removed from modern day reality. 「Q40」 On the other hand, Stevenson’s skill with plotting and narrative give his books a timeless quality, so that they still live today. 「Q39」 And Stevenson was also the shrewder judge of behaviour and psychology. For example, his compelling description of a man with a split personality in the Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde has proved so accessible and accurate that the expression ‘Jekyll and Hyde’ has entered common English usage. 「Q36」 Even if we do not see a revival of critical interest in this great Scottish writer, it is to be hoped that readers go back to Robert Louis Stevenson’s magnificent stories and reassess the neglected genius. 
E  评价史蒂文森的生平与创作，有一个问题始终无法回避：他能否称得上苏格兰最伟大的英语散文作家？多数评论家将这一殊荣归于《艾凡赫》等经典名著的作者沃尔特・司各特爵士。单论写作技法、文字精准度，以及把控语言营造意境的功底，司各特确实更胜一筹。但技法精湛并不等同于天赋卓绝。不妨类比俄罗斯两位作曲大师肖斯塔科维奇与普罗科菲耶夫：前者演奏技法更为严谨精细，后者却拥有更纯粹、更高的艺术天赋。司各特与史蒂文森亦是如此。不可否认，司各特细腻繁复的文风，让他的作品能够刻画史蒂文森难以企及的悲剧层次，但他的文字透着尘封博物馆的陈旧气息，刻意雕琢，脱离现代现实。反观史蒂文森，精妙的情节构思与叙事笔法，赋予了作品永恒的生命力，至今仍被大众品读。同时，他对人性行为与心理的洞察也更为敏锐通透。例如《化身博士》中对人格分裂者的刻画，生动传神、精准贴切，“杰基尔与海德” 甚至成为英语通用俗语。即便文学评论界不再重新掀起研究这位苏格兰文学巨匠的热潮，也希望读者能重读史蒂文森的经典佳作，重新审视这位被埋没的天才。
