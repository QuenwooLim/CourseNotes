Bovids

牛科动物

A The family of mammals called bovids belongs to the Artiodactyl class. Bovids are a highly diverse group consisting of 137 species, some of which are man's most important domestic animals.

A 牛科动物属于偶蹄目。牛科是一个高度多样化的群体，包含137个物种，其中一些是人类最重要的家养动物。


B Bovids are found in most parts of Eurasia and Southeast Asian Islands. 「Q28」 However, they are the greatest in number and the most diverse in the Southeast Asian Islands. While some Bovid species are solitary animals, others are social and live in groups. 「Q29」 Although Bovids are well adapted to a wide array of habitats, a majority of them prefer open grassland, deserts or scrubs. This habitat flexibility matches the difference in body size and form, 「Q36」 ranging from the royal antelope of West Africa which is just 25cm at shoulder height to the massively built Bison of Europe and North America that has a shoulder height of 2.2m.
B 牛科动物广泛分布于欧亚大陆大部分地区以及东南亚岛屿。然而，它们在东南亚岛屿的数量最多，种类也最丰富。有些牛科动物是独居的，而另一些则是群居动物。尽管牛科动物能适应多种多样的栖息环境，但大多数偏好开阔的草原、沙漠或灌木地带。这种栖息地的灵活性与其体型和形态的差异相匹配，从小至肩高仅25厘米的西非皇家羚羊，到体型庞大的欧洲和北美洲野牛（肩高可达2.2米）均有分布。


C Some features are common to all Bovids despite the size and form diversity. 「Q30」 All species are ruminants, which means that they retain undigested food in their stomachs, and regurgitate it as necessary. Bovids are almost exclusively grass eaters.
C 尽管体型和形态差异显著，但所有牛科动物都具有一些共同特征。所有物种都是反刍动物，这意味着它们会将未完全消化的食物储存在胃中，并在需要时反刍咀嚼。牛科动物几乎以草为食。


D Typically their teeth are highly modified for browsing and grazing: grass or foliage is cropped with the upper lip and lower incisors (the upper incisors are usually absent), and then ground down by the cheek teeth. The males of all bovid species and the females of most bovid species not only have cloven (split) hooves, they also have horns. With bony cores covered in a sheath of horn material that is continually regenerated from within, bovid horns are unbranched and never shed. They differ in both shape and size. For example, the horns of a large Indian buffalo can measure up to 4m along the outer curve, from one tip to the other, while different kinds of gazelles display a variety of exquisite curves.

D 通常，它们的牙齿高度特化，适合采食和研磨植物：利用上唇与下门齿切断草或树叶（上门齿通常缺失），再用颊齿将其磨碎。所有牛科动物的雄性以及大多数雌性不仅具有分趾蹄（即偶蹄），还长有角。这些角由骨质核心构成，外覆不断从内部再生的角质鞘，不分叉且终生不脱落。角的形状和大小各异，例如印度大水牛的角沿外缘测量可达4米（从一端到另一端），而不同种类的瞪羚则展现出各种精巧的弯曲形态。


E Antelope, Antilocapridae, Bovinae, Caprinae, and Cephalophinae form the five sub-families or groups that can be differentiated. 「Q32」 The bigger bovids include bison, cattle, eland, nilgai, and the African bongo, and together they form the Bovinae sub-family. 「Q37」 The auroch, forerunner of Europe's domestic cattle, is extinct, and the water buffalo, yak, gaur, and banteng all descend from ancestors that are rarely found in the wild, or appear on endangered lists.
E 羚羊、叉角羚科、牛亚科、羊亚科和小羚亚科构成了五个可区分的亚科或类群。体型较大的牛科动物包括野牛、家牛、大羚羊、蓝牛羚和非洲邦戈羚，它们共同组成牛亚科。野牛是欧洲家牛的祖先，现已灭绝；水牛、牦牛、印度野牛和爪哇野牛的野生祖先如今也极为罕见，或已被列入濒危物种名单。


F ‘Antelope’ is a loose zoological term used to describe a group of bovids that have followed different lines of evolution 「Q34」 . A species that is distinguished by its long-legged and fast-running characteristics, which is the antelope, often has a long horn that may be laid along the back when the animal is in full flight. There are two major subfamilies of antelope: The Hippotraginae, a group which includes the oryx and the addax, and the Antilpinae, a group of generally smaller and more graceful animals, such as the gazelle and the springbok. Although antelopes are mostly a grassland species, over the years they have adapted their habitat to include flooded grasslands. Many species, including pukus, waterbucks, and lechwes, are good swimmers and often feed in deep water. 「Q38」 The sitatunga has long splayed hooves that allow it to walk easily in swampy terrain.
F “羚羊”是一个较为宽泛的动物学术语，用来描述在进化过程中走向不同方向的一类牛科动物。以长腿善跑为特征的羚羊，通常具有长角，在全速奔跑时可将角贴于背部。羚羊主要有两大类群：Hippotraginae类群包括大羚羊和旋角羚；Antilpinae类群则包含体型较小、姿态优雅的动物，如瞪羚和跳羚。虽然羚羊主要生活在草原，但多年来也适应了包括洪泛草原在内的多种环境。许多物种，如瓦氏赤羚、水羚和驴羚，擅长游泳，常在深水中觅食。林羚则具有细长且分叉的蹄，使其能在沼泽地形中轻松行走。


G The sub-family Caprinae includes the sheep and the goat, together with various relatives such as the goral and the tahr. Most are woolly or have long hair. Several species, such as wild goats, chamois and ibex, are agile cliff and mountain dwellers. Tolerance of extreme conditions is most marked in this group: 「Q39」 「Q31」 Barbary and bighorn sheep have adapted to arid deserts, while Rocky Mountain sheep survive high up in mountains and musk oxen in arctic tundra.
G 羊亚科包括绵羊和山羊，以及如斑羚、塔尔羊等近亲物种。多数体表被毛浓密或长有长毛。一些物种，如野山羊、岩羚和羱羊，善于在悬崖峭壁间活动。该类群对极端环境的耐受性尤为突出：巴巴里绵羊和大角羊适应了干旱沙漠，而落基山雪羊能在高海拔山区生存，麝牛则栖息于北极苔原。


H The duiker of Africa belongs to the Cephalophinas sub-family. It is generally small and solitary, often living in thick forest. 「Q33」 Although mainly feeding on grass and leaves, some duikers, unlike most other bovids, are believed to eat insects, feed on dead animal carcasses, and even to kill small animals.
H 非洲小羚羊属于小羚亚科。它们通常体型较小，独居生活，多栖息于密林之中。虽然主要以草和树叶为食，但与大多数其他牛科动物不同，部分小羚羊被认为会食用昆虫、腐肉，甚至捕杀小型动物。


I 「Q40」 The pronghorn is the sole survivor of a New World sub-family of herbivorous ruminants, the Antilocapridae in North America. It is similar in appearance and habits to the Old World antelope. Although greatly reduced in numbers since the arrival of Europeans, and the subsequent enclosure of grasslands, the pronghorn is still found in considerable numbers throughout North America, from Washington State to Mexico. When alarmed by the approach of wolves or other predators, hairs on the pronghorn's rump stand erect, revealing a white patch.

I 叉角羚是新大陆一个草食性反刍动物亚科中唯一的现存物种，分布于北美洲。其外形和习性与旧大陆的羚羊相似。尽管自欧洲人到来后，由于草原被圈占，其数量大幅下降，但叉角羚至今仍在从华盛顿州到墨西哥的广大北美地区广泛分布。当察觉到狼或其他捕食者接近时，叉角羚臀部的毛发会竖起，暴露出白色的斑块以示警戒。


J Despite the many different species of bovids, their social organisation can be categorized as either unsocial or social, and their mating system can be categorized as monogamous and territorial, polygynous and territorial, or based on a male dominance hierarchy. Furthermore, there is a clear dichotomy between bovids that live in closed habitats (e.g. forest and bush) and those that live in open habitats (e.g. plains and mountains). Through the process of convergence, species of different lineages that have adapted for similar habitats come to share a number of correlated traits. Thus, closed-habitat bovids have a body plan that is adapted for moving in dense undergrowth and they rely on hiding and concealing coloration to avoid predators. Open-habitat bovids do not hide except in early infancy and they have a build adapted for flight in the open. They have a conspicuous and distinctive coloration that advertises their presence and species.

J‌ 尽管牛科动物的物种非常多样，但它们的社会组织可以分为“非群居”和“群居”两类，而其交配系统则可分为：一夫一妻且具领地性、一夫多妻且具领地性，或基于雄性等级优势的体系。此外，生活在封闭栖息地（如森林和灌木丛）的牛科动物与生活在开阔栖息地（如草原和山地）的牛科动物之间存在明显分化。通过趋同演化，不同演化支系中适应相似栖息地的物种，逐渐发展出一系列相关联的特征。因此，生活在封闭栖息地的牛科动物拥有适应在茂密下层植被中活动的体型结构，它们依赖躲藏和隐蔽的体色来躲避捕食者。而生活在开阔栖息地的牛科动物除了幼年早期外并不躲藏，其身体结构适应在开阔地带快速奔跑。它们具有醒目而独特的体色，用以昭示自身的存在和物种身份。


K Whether the mating system is territorial or based on a male dominance hierarchy may be linked to phylogeny. 「Q35」 The members of the subfamily Bovinae, which appear to have separated from the main bovid line very early, are virtually all non-territorial. For the rest, the Antilopinae and the duiker tribe, breeding males are territorial. All African bovids bear single young, whereas twins are common among the Antilopini, Caprini, and Boselaphini of the Northern Hemisphere.

K‌ 交配系统是基于领地还是雄性等级优势，可能与系统发育有关。牛亚科成员似乎很早就从主干牛科谱系中分离出来，它们几乎都不具领地行为。而对于其他类群，如羚羊亚科和小羚羊族，繁殖期的雄性则具有领地性。所有非洲牛科动物通常每胎只产一仔，而北半球的羚羊族、羊族和牛羚族则常见双胞胎。
