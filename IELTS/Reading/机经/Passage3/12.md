Early Exploration of the West of Canada

加拿大西部的早期探险
A   By the mid-1870s, most of Canada's population was in the eastern part of the country 「Q28」 . The western part of the country was sparsely populated, with no major cities or rural settlement areas 「Q29」 . As a result, the federal government implemented a plan to encourage agricultural settlement in the West 「Q30」 . Western rural settlement was desired, as the eastern agricultural land was already nearly filled to capacity and the large expanses of available land in the West offered great agricultural opportunities, including opportunities for Canada to grow its economy.

到19世纪70年代中期，加拿大的大部分人口都居住在东部地区。 「Q28」 国家的西部地区人口稀少，没有大城市，也没有农村居民点。 「Q29」 因此，联邦政府实施了一项鼓励西部农业定居的计划。 「Q30」 西部的农村定居是需要的，因为东部的农业用地已经几乎填满，西部的大片可用土地为发展农业提供了许多机会，包括加拿大发展经济的机会。
B   In an effort to get people to settle in the West and to increase the country's agricultural output, the Canadian government took measures to secure land there 「Q31」 . The Dominion of Canada purchased Rupert's Land from the Hudson's Bay Company in 1870. 「Q32」 Soon after in 1872, the federal government enacted the Dominion Lands Act, an act that enabled settlers to acquire 160 acres of free land as long as they remained on the land for a minimum of three years, made specific minor improvements to the land, and paid a $10 registration fee at the beginning. The government also created a Mounted Police Force in 1873 and then sent the Mounties west to secure areas for the future settlers. The initiatives implemented by the government worked, attracting settlers not only from Eastern Canada, but also from Europe and the 「Q33」 US. The area of Manitoba was an area that particularly attracted an influx of people with the desire to start a new life.

为了让人们在西部定居并增加本国的农业产量，加拿大政府采取了措施来获取那里的土地。 「Q31」 1870年，加拿大自治领从哈德逊湾公司购买了鲁伯特的土地。 「Q32」 不久之后在1872年，联邦政府颁布了《自治领土地法案》，此法案使移民获得160英亩的免费土地，前提是他们仍在该土地上开垦至少三年，对土地进行具体的小改进，并在一开始支付10加元的注册费。政府还在1873年建立了骑警部队，然后把骑警派到西部保护未来的定居者的地区。政府实施的计划奏效了，不仅吸引了加拿大东部的定居者，也吸引了欧洲和美国的定居者。 「Q33」 马尼托巴省的这个地区特别吸引了大批渴望开始新生活的人。
C   The Canadian government had decided that the sure-fire way to protect the Canadian territory and to eventually achieve the goal of joining British Columbia with the rest of the country was to get large numbers of people to settle in the West. The settling of the West would then make the construction of a transcontinental railway a necessity； once that was established，the West could ship its agricultural products to feed the burgeoning urban industrial areas of the East. In turn, the East could ship its manufactured products to the West, creating a vibrant East-West economy.

加拿大政府已经决定，保护加拿大领土并最终实现把不列颠哥伦比亚省加入加拿大其他地区的目标的可靠方法是让大量的人在西部定居。定居西部会使建设一条横贯大陆的铁路成为必要；一旦该铁路建立起来，西部就可以将其农产品运往东方新兴的城市工业区。反过来，东部也可以将其制造的产品运往西部，从而创造出一个充满活力的东西部经济。

D  「Q34」  During this immigration period, Winnipeg became the metropolis of the West. Its growth resulted from land speculation, a growth in housing, and the federal government's decision in 1881 to make Winnipeg a major stop along the Canadian Pacific Railway (CPR) . With this decision, Winnipeg stood at the junction of three transcontinental railway lines which subsequently employed thousands of workers. With its access to transportation, Winnipeg also became the major processor of agricultural products for the surrounding areas.

「Q34」 在这段移民时期，温尼伯成为西部大都市。它的发展源于土地投机、住房的发展，以及1881年联邦政府让温尼伯成为加拿大太平洋铁路沿线的一个主要站点的决定。作出这一决定后，温尼伯处在三条横贯大陆的铁路线的交界处，这三条铁路线随后雇用了数以千计的工人。由于交通便利，温尼伯也成为周边地区主要的农产品加工中心。

E   Most of the people who settled in and around Winnipeg during this period were Protestant English-speaking settlers from Ontario and the British Isles. These settlers established a culture that was based on the British-Ontarian ethos from which they had come 「Q35」 . The ethnic homogeneity of the area did not last very long, however, as increasing numbers of foreign immigrants, especially from Austria-Hungary and Ukraine, soon added a new ethnic element to the region. Settling the West with Eastern Canadians and British immigrants provided Canada with a safeguard from the threat of an American takeover, especially after the Minnesota legislature in the US passed a resolution which provided for the annexation of the fertile Red River area, at the time the most important settlement on the Canadian prairies.

在此期间，居住在温尼伯及其周围的大多数人都是来自安大略省和不列颠群岛讲英语的新教徒。这些移民建立了一种基于不列颠安大略精神的文化。 「Q35」 然而，该地区的种族同质性并没有持续很长时间，因为外国移民，特别是来自奥匈帝国和乌克兰的移民的快速增长很快给该地区增加了一个新的种族元素。让东部加拿大人和英国移民去西部定居的这种做法为加拿大提供了保证，使其免受美国接管的威胁，尤其是在美国的明尼苏达州议会通过一项使吞并肥沃的红河地区有据可依的决议之后，这个地区是当时加拿大草原最重要的定居点。

F   Not all of those who settled in Western Canada in the 1880s wished to remain there， especially after the economic depression of the 1870s and 1880s. This depression kept the value of Canada's main exports low, including agriculture, at a time when many other countries were competing for immigrant settleme 「Q36」 nt. Countries including the US, Brazil，Argentina, Australia, and New Zealand were all open to immigrants and were competing with Canada for those immigrants. As a result, many immigrants opted instead to settle the American frontier in the US. These American settlers included many Canadi 「Q39」 ans. Before 1891，Canada was described as "a huge demographic railway station", in which large numbers of people came and went, with departures often outnumbering arrivals.

并不是所有在19世纪80年代定居加拿大西部的人都希望留在那里，尤其是在19世纪70年代和80年代的经济萧条之后。经济大萧条使加拿大包括农业在内的主要出口国家正在为移民定居而竞争。 「Q36」 包括美国、巴西、阿根廷、澳大利亚和新西兰在内的国家都对移民开放，并与加拿大争抢这些移民。因此，许多移民选择在美国边远地区定居。这些美国移民包括许多加拿大人。 「Q39」 1891年以前的加拿大被描述为“一个巨大的人口火车站”，大量的人口在这里进进出出，离开的人数往往超过到达的人数。
G   By 1891, Eastern Canada had its share of large urban areas and the problems associated with large urban areas. Although the wealthy areas of Toronto and Montreal were wired for electricity and telephones, there were also areas in those cities that had slum conditions where livestock ran on the streets and human waste spilled into public area 「Q37」 s. Social reformers were quick to encourage some of these impoverished city dwellers to move to the rural areas, where they could enjoy the fresh air and the open spaces, becoming “true Canadians”. Agriculture played an integral role in the Canadian economy in 1891, as one-third of the workforce worked on farms.

到1891年，加拿大东部已经有了大片的城市地区和与大片城市地区相关的问题。虽然多伦多和蒙特利尔的富裕地区已经通了电和电话，但在这些城市中也有一些地区存在牲畜横行街头和人类排泄物流入公共区域的贫民窟。 「Q37」 社会改革者很快鼓励这些贫困的城市居民搬到农村地区，在那里他们可以享受新鲜的空气和开放的空间，成为“真正的加拿大人”。1891年，农业在加拿大经济中扮演了不可或缺的角色，因为当时三分之一的劳动力都在农场工作。

H  「Q40」  In its attempt to get overseas immigrants to settle in Canada, the Canadian government used a number of incentives, including free or cheap land to potential farmers. Through government agencies, Canada offered assisted passage plans, with bonuses and commissions to both agents and settlers. Also, letters sent overseas from settlers already in Canada to friends and family members often served as successful recruiting tools. Many of these letters painted a rosy picture of the new world, as many settlers already in Canada did not want to disappoint or worry family or friends from their homeland 「Q38」 . So, embellished tales of wonder, good fortune, and happiness often succeeded in encouraging others to immigrate from their home countries to Canada.

「Q40」 为了让海外移民在加拿大定居，加拿大政府采取了一系列激励措施，包括为潜在的农民提供免费或廉价的土地。通过政府机构，加拿大提供了协助通过计划，向代理机构和定居者都提供奖金和佣金。此外，已经在加拿大定居的人寄给朋友和家人的海外信件也常常成为成功的招募工具。许多信件描绘了新世界的美好图景，因为许多已经在加拿大定居 「Q38」 的人不想让家乡的家人或朋友失望或担忧。因此，美化的关于奇迹、好运和幸福的故事往往能成功地鼓励其他人从他们的祖国移民到加拿大。
