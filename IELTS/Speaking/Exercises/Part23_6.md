# 25. 很久没收到回复的信息

## P2 故事

### Who

* a professor
* a research supervisor
* university teacher

导师/教授

### Message about

* summer research opportunity
* academic visit
* research project

暑研或科研项目

### What happened

* sent an email
* waited for nearly two weeks
* checked my inbox every day

发邮件后等了两周

### Finally?

* yes
* professor was busy
* received a positive reply

最终收到了回复

### Feelings

开始：

* worried
* nervous
* uncertain

后来：

* relieved
* excited
* grateful

---

## 万能句

> At first, I thought the professor had ignored my email, but it turned out that he was simply busy with academic work.

起初我以为教授忽略了我的邮件，后来发现他只是太忙了。

---

## P3关键词

### Why reply slowly?

* busy schedule
* too many messages
* forget accidentally

工作忙、消息太多、忘记回复

### Why no reply?

* not interested
* spam messages
* no useful information

### Message vs Call

* less disturbing
* convenient
* reply anytime

信息不会打扰别人。

### Respect in messages

* polite language
* greetings
* thank the receiver

礼貌表达。
