# 1. Music

### Do you prefer sad or happy music?

I prefer happy music because **I'm quite an optimistic person**. Listening to **upbeat songs** gives me a sense of relaxation and enjoyment. Whenever **I'm under pressure from my studies**, happy music helps me improve my mood and stay positive.

### Does happy music make you feel more excited?

Yes, definitely. Happy music can instantly **lift my mood** and **make me feel more energetic**. For example, when I listen to upbeat pop songs, I often feel motivated and ready to focus on my work or studies.

### Have you taken any music classes?

No, I haven't. I enjoy listening to music in my free time, but **I've never learned how to sing or play an instrument**. To be honest, music theory is Greek to me, so I don't know much about things like rhythm or melody.

### Do you listen to music while doing other things?

Yes, quite often. I usually listen to music **while studying, doing housework, or walking around campus**. It helps me relax and stay focused, especially when I'm doing repetitive tasks that might otherwise feel boring.
